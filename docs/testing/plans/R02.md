# R02 Customer account test plan

**Slice:** R02 · Customer account (EC-64). **Decision:** D74. **Done when:** the open balance equals contract totals − payments − returns.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/r02_customer_account_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/r02_customer_account_test.sql
```

Sales, contracts, schedule lines, payments, allocations, returns, refunds, store credit, fees and waivers are seeded as the database owner (nothing writes contracts or collections yet: N02–N05).

- **Open balance (Done when):** two active contracts (12 000 and 7 200), 3 000 collected: 16 200, the same as the views' remaining; a completed contract adds 0 and a reversed collection does not count; a contract-adjustment return of 1 200 → 19 200 − 3 000 − 1 200 = 15 000; returns refunded in cash or to store credit are not contract returns; a customer with nothing owes 0; another shop sees nothing.
- **Contract total:** equals `total_payable` for a contract never rescheduled; a rescheduled one = current lines 4 200 + 1 000 paid on superseded lines = 5 200, open 4 200 = the views.
- **Fees:** 150 charged, 50 waived → 100 due; total due = open balance + 100; not in the open balance.
- **Overdue:** lines 10, 45 and 100 days late in their buckets and one not yet due; 3 000 on three lines, 100 days, next due = the oldest unpaid line; after it is paid the next due moves and the 90+ bucket empties; zeros and nulls without contracts.
- **Account:** the balance block (open, fees, total due, store credit, R03 limit and available); the head has no national ID; every contract with its balances; totals skip a voided sale, count every return and leave store credit out; recent payments are effective (a reversed one and its reversal are left out), newest first, at most 10, with what they paid; recent returns with their sale; documents on the customer and its contracts; a quiet customer reads with empty lists.
- **Schedule:** current lines first, then superseded ones; paid, overdue and upcoming lines with days late only when overdue; the fee with its waiver and what is due; the contract block equals the account's; another customer's or an unknown contract → `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND`.
- **Statement:** the whole history of a customer with a cash sale, an installment sale with a bank-transfer down payment, a collection, a fee and its waiver, a voided sale refunded in cash, a return refunded to store credit and a later sale paid with it, in order with the running balance; closing = total due − store credit (3 560), and the same check for every customer of the test; store-credit payments and refunds add no row; a void is a return row; a payment row carries its method and bank reference; a reversal undoes its payment; a contract without a sale adds its total; a range (opening, 7 rows, closing, totals); only a start date; a quiet customer; `from > to` and more than three years → `VALIDATION_FAILED`, exactly three years is fine.
- **Who and tenancy:** a cashier reads all three; a warehouse user → `PERMISSION_DENIED`; shop B gets `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND` for shop A's customer (and the other way round); a Starter shop → `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN`; an expired shop still reads.
- **Grants:** the helpers are not executable by `authenticated`; exactly the three public functions are granted (not to anon); no audit row.
- **Other suites:** `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases for the three functions, on a migrated contract of the R03 denial customer), `security_guards_test.sql` (the three reads allowlisted).

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/customerAccount.test.ts`: a full and an empty account, money as a number and an unknown contract status refused, a migrated contract without a sale, the schedule (statuses), the schedule params, every statement row kind, the statement query (optional dates, order, three years, no other keys), `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND` 404.
- `src/services/customerAccount.service.test.ts`: RPC names and arguments (dates left out when absent), answers parsed with the contracts, `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND` / `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND` 404 and `VALIDATION_FAILED` 400 mapped.
- `src/routes/customerAccount.integration.test.ts` (local stack): a fresh shop sells to one customer in cash and by bank transfer through the real API; the account shows 2 purchases, 500 in and nothing owed; the statement rows are sale, payment, sale, payment and close at 0; a range of today has the 4 rows; an unknown contract → `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND`; a backwards and a too-long range → 400; an unknown customer → 404; a warehouse user → 403 on all three.
- Cross-shop (224 routes): the account and the statement refuse another shop's customer, a foreign header and an org query key; the schedule refuses another shop's customer and a foreign header.
