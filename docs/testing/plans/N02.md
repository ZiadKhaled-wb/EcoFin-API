# N02 Installment sale and contract test plan

**Slice:** N02 · Installment sale and contract (EC-81). **Decision:** D77. **Rules:** `docs/ISLAMIC_FINANCE_RULES.md`. **Done when:** the contract total = cash price − down payment + markup; the down payment is in the drawer; the contract shows on the customer account (R02).

## SQL (`supabase/tests/n02_installment_sale_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/n02_installment_sale_test.sql
```

Shops a (retail_pro, VAT-inclusive prices), b (retail) and c (starter); in shop a an owner, a cashier with an open shift on a paired device, a warehouse user and a cashier of a second branch; customers with a national ID and a limit, without a limit, blocked, without an ID, with a blank ID; shop B's customer. The policy (10 % up to 6 months, 20 % up to 24, 10 % minimum down payment, a guarantor above 20 000 financed, a survey above 30 000) is inserted as the database owner. After every sale the commit-time triggers of R03, R04, R05 and N01 are fired with `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL IMMEDIATE`.

- **Done when:** 12 000.00 with 2 000.00 down, 12 monthly at the 20 % tier → financed 10 000.00, profit 2 000.00, total 12 000.00 = cash − down + profit; 12 lines of 1 000.00 that add up (amount, principal, profit) and equal the engine's lines; one cash movement of 2 000.00 into the shift's drawer; `v_sale_balances` paid 2 000, financed 10 000, unpaid 0; R02's account lists the contract and the open balance and statement close at 12 000.00.
- **The contract:** CT number, active, linked to the sale, `in_house` creditor, the request's key, created by the cashier, on the current policy version; the answer carries the sale and the contract with its lines and balance; audit `sale.created` (payments = the down payment) and `contract.created` (info).
- **Idempotency:** the same key returns the same contract and writes nothing; a key a cash sale used → `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.
- **Refusals (nothing written, no number taken):** no customer, an unknown key in the body, no policy version, a repeated guarantor → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; unknown or shop B's customer → `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`; blocked → `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`; no or blank national ID → `CUSTOMER_NATIONAL_ID_REQUIRED`; over the limit → `CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`; an unknown approval → `CREDIT_APPROVAL_INVALID`; above 20 000 without a guarantor → `GUARANTOR_REQUIRED {required 1, given 0}`; an unknown guarantor → `GUARANTOR_NOT_FOUND`; a broken rule → `INSTALLMENT_TERMS_INVALID` with the violations; an old policy version → `POLICY_CHANGED {current_version}`; a different cash total → `PRICE_CHANGED`; down payment lines that don't add up → `PAYMENT_TOTAL_MISMATCH`; out of stock → `OUT_OF_STOCK`.
- **Shop-level refusals:** installments off → `INSTALLMENTS_DISABLED`; profit taxed → `MARKUP_VAT_NOT_SUPPORTED`; a warehouse user → `PERMISSION_DENIED`; not an employee → `NOT_AN_EMPLOYEE`; the owner without a shift → `SHIFT_REQUIRED`; a starter shop → `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN`; an expired shop → `SUBSCRIPTION_EXPIRED`; a revoked device → `DEVICE_REVOKED`; nothing written.
- **Credit, guarantors, survey:** an approved exception lets a customer over the limit buy, its `entity_id` is the contract, the audit row is a warning, a second use → `CREDIT_APPROVAL_INVALID`; a linked guarantor is attached and answered with its relation; above 30 000 without an approved survey → `SURVEY_REQUIRED`, with one it passes.
- **Variants:** a bank transfer down payment goes to the bank; a store credit down payment lowers the balance; weekly × 4 and daily × 45; a 5 % discounted cart (cash price = discounted total); a month-end first due date; a zero down payment (a newer policy with 0 % minimum) posts no payment and payment lines are refused.
- **Policy change and never edited:** a policy published after a contract leaves it and its lines unchanged; the next contract uses the new version; updating a term or a line is refused; every contract adds up, every sale is settled, cash balances equal their ledgers.
- **Read:** the cashier reads the contract; unknown id, another branch, shop B → `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND`; a warehouse user → `PERMISSION_DENIED`; an expired shop can read.
- **Grants:** the `app` helpers and the new core signature are not executable by `authenticated`; the two public functions are.
- **Other suites:** `s01`/`s02`/`s03` (the core's new parameter changes nothing for cash sales; S01's grants check names the new signature), `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases for both functions, the owner's same calls work), `security_guards_test.sql` (`get_contract` allowlisted as a read), `ledger_invariants_test.sql` (`post_installment_sale` in the replay list), and R02/R03/R04/R05/N01 unchanged.

## Contracts and API

```text
npm run test:contracts
npx vitest run src/services/installments.service.test.ts
npm run check:cross-shop && npm run check:audit && npm run check:permissions
```

- Contracts: `InstallmentSaleBody` (a customer and the policy version are required; unknown keys, a zero cash price, a duplicate or a fourth guarantor refused; `guarantor_ids` defaults to `[]`), `Contract` (a profit % with more than 4 decimals refused).
- Service: one RPC with the body and the key; the sale answers with its receipt part; a database code maps to its error; a wrong shape is refused; the read answers `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND` as 404.
- Cross-shop: the sale route refuses a foreign header (403), an injected `organization_id` (400) and shop B's customer (`CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`), with no audit row; the read refuses a foreign header and answers `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND`.
