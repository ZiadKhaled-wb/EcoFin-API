# R03 Credit profile and limit test plan

**Slice:** R03 · Credit profile and limit (EC-65). **Decision:** D72. **Done when:** the server blocks an installment sale over the limit even if the client sends it directly.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/r03_credit_profile_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/r03_credit_profile_test.sql
```

- **Limit:** a new customer has 0 and nothing available; set by an owner and a manager (`credit.approve`); a cashier, a collector and a warehouse user are refused; raising needs a reason of 3 or more (none, two letters, blanks → `CREDIT_REASON_REQUIRED`; 301 letters → `VALIDATION_FAILED`), lowering does not; negative, over 99 999 999.99, three decimals, text and null → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; the same value writes no audit row; the maximum is allowed; an archived customer → `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED` but is still readable; `update_customer` still refuses `credit_limit`; another shop's or an unknown customer → `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`.
- **History:** newest first with from, to, who and the reason; only the last 10 of 11 changes; audit rows hold before and after, a warning when raising.
- **Suggested limit:** income 8 000 x multiple 3 = 24 000; null without income or without the multiple; 8 000.50 x 2.5 rounds down to 20 001; never used by the check.
- **Exposure** (contracts, lines and payments seeded as the database owner): two open contracts with 16 000 principal and 2 000 paid → 14 000; a completed contract and a superseded line don't count; a rescheduled one counts and a written-off one does not; the same number as `v_customer_exposure`; another shop sees nothing; a limit below the exposure is allowed and the profile shows 0 available and the excess.
- **Score:** 2 guarantors + income + ID + photo = 80 (components and weights in order); no guarantors = 40; one guarantor = 20; three are capped like two; nothing = 0; an invalid national ID gives only the photo's half; an archived guarantor doesn't count; the shop's custom weights and minimum score are used (half up: 37.5 → 38).
- **Behaviour:** one line on time, one 5 days late, one overdue and one not yet due → 3 due lines, 33 % on time, 10 days late, 1 000.00 overdue, 1 overdue line; paid in two halves with the last 3 days late = late, paid on the due date = on time, a completed contract counts, a superseded line doesn't (67 %, 3 days); a customer without contracts has nulls and zeros.
- **Check (Done when):** limit 20 000 with 14 000 owed: 6 000.00 passes (null), 6 000.01 → `CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` with limit, exposure, available, requested, score, min_score and the reasons; a blocked or archived customer → `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED` even with an approval; a watch customer → `CREDIT_CUSTOMER_WATCH`; a score below the minimum → `CREDIT_SCORE_TOO_LOW`; both limit and score → the limit error listing both; a new customer (limit 0) needs an exception; another shop's customer and a missing amount are refused; an approved exception for at least the amount passes and returns its id (also for watch and score); for a smaller amount, another customer, rejected, cancelled, pending, an unknown id, a discount approval, another shop, expired (25 hours) or already used → `CREDIT_APPROVAL_INVALID`; 23 hours is still good; when nothing is needed the approval is ignored.
- **Exceptions:** request (a cashier) → pending with the snapshot, the reasons, who asked and `mine`; reason rules, amount rules, `APPROVAL_NOT_NEEDED`, a blocked or archived or other shop's customer; decide by a manager (a cashier is refused; a rejection needs a note; a decided one can't be decided again; a manager can't decide their own request → `APPROVAL_SELF`; the OWNER can); cancel only by the requester while pending; the list (pending first, by status, all, a bad status); a discount approval is not found by the credit functions and the other way round; the freeze trigger lets a used exception take its contract id once and refuses everything else (and S03's discount approval still takes its sale id once).
- **Preview:** nothing pending before a request; the pending request shows for an amount it covers and not for a larger one; the approved exception shows with its id and turns `allowed` on; 9 000 against an exception of 8 000 still needs approval.
- **Trigger (Done when):** an over-limit contract with no approval fails at commit with `CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (details with the exposure left out of the new contract) and leaves nothing behind; within the limit, a migrated contract, and an over-limit one with an exception used for it (also one approved for more) commit; the same exception can't be used for a second contract and a second over-limit contract fails; an exception for less than the contract fails; a blocked customer fails; an exception of another customer can't be used; the new contracts add no exposure (no lines).
- **Who, plan, expired shop, tenancy, grants:** a warehouse user can't read or ask; a collector reads; a Starter shop → `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN` on all eight; an expired shop reads but can't write; shop B gets not found for every customer and approval of shop A and lists none; the helpers (`check_customer_credit`, `use_credit_approval`, the score) are not executable by `authenticated`; no direct table writes (customers, approvals); exactly the eight public functions are granted.
- **Audit:** one row per action with entity type `customer` (limit) or `approval`; no 14-digit number anywhere.
- **Other suites:** `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases for the eight functions), `security_guards_test.sql` (the four reads allowlisted), `s03_discounts_test.sql` (the freeze trigger it relies on).

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/credit.test.ts`: the reasons, the score component, the behaviour with nulls, the profile (four components, ten history rows), the limit body (0, maximum, pence, negatives, three decimals, unknown keys), the check query (above zero), the check, exceptions (pending, approved, used), list query and status, request and decide bodies, the detail shape and the five error codes.
- `src/services/credit.service.test.ts`: RPC arguments (reason and note left out when absent), the answers parsed with the contracts, error mapping with the details of the three credit errors and the safe log.
- `src/routes/credit.integration.test.ts` (local stack): the owner sets the multiple and a limit (reason required to raise), reads the profile; a cashier and a collector read it, the cashier gets 403 on the limit route and on decide; the cashier asks for an exception, the owner approves it (24 hours), the preview shows `approved_approval_id`; cancel only by the requester; a rejection needs a note; bad bodies, queries and ids are 400.
- Cross-shop (221 routes): the 8 routes refuse a foreign header and an org id; shop B's customer and exception are not found, not listed, not decided or cancelled, and stay unchanged. Audit drivers for the 4 writes.
