# S02 Split payments test plan

**Slice:** S02 · Split payments (EC-69). **Decision:** D64. **Done when:** a sale paid half cash, half bank transfer posts each amount to the right place and the shift shows both (K04's day close will sum the same rows).

## SQL (`supabase/tests/s02_split_payments_test.sql`, 62 cases)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/s02_split_payments_test.sql
```

- **Setup:** a shop with two branches; 60 chairs (100.00) and 5 sofas (380.00); banks: shop-wide NBE, CIB of the main branch, Ahli of the second branch, an inactive one; a customer with 300.00 of store credit (seeded in the ledger); another shop with its own bank and customer; a cashier and a warehouse user.
- **The account list:** empty without an open shift; with one: the branch's active bank and the shop-wide one by name, no balances, no safe, drawer, other branch or inactive bank; a warehouse user is refused.
- **Quote:** no payments = paid in full; half cash (600.00 handed over) + half bank: paid, remaining 0.00, change 100.00; an incomplete list reports what remains; the store credit balance is reported; store credit without a customer and another shop's bank are refused; nothing written.
- **Done when (half and half):** 1 000.00 paid 500.00 cash and 500.00 by transfer with reference `TRX-1` -> two payments (PY) with allocations and keys `system:sale:<id>:n`; the drawer and the shift's expected cash up by exactly 500.00; the bank up by 500.00 with a `sale` movement without `shift_id`; the shift shows cash 500.00, transfer 500.00 and sales total 1 000.00; the audit row lists the lines.
- **Idempotent:** the same key again answers the same sale and payments, even with another cart; nothing is posted twice.
- **No payments:** one cash payment of the total; an explicit JSON `null` is the same.
- **Cash with change:** amount 380.00, tendered 400.00 -> tendered stored, change 20.00, the drawer up by 380.00 only; tendered below the amount refused.
- **Store credit:** more than the balance -> `INSUFFICIENT_STORE_CREDIT` with the balance; no customer -> `VALIDATION_FAILED`; 300.00 of credit + 700.00 cash -> the balance is 0.00 with one -300.00 movement; the next sale on credit is refused with balance 0.00.
- **Several transfers:** three lines into two banks, the reference trimmed, in order; the banks hold 1 100.00 and 400.00; the shift shows cash, transfers and credit separately and its expected cash counts the cash only.
- **Refused, nothing changes** (the footprint of sales, stock rows, payments, allocations, cash rows, store credit rows, counters and audit rows is the same): paid less and paid more (`PAYMENT_TOTAL_MISMATCH` with both amounts), an amount of 0, two cash lines, two credit lines, four transfers, six lines, an empty list, no or blank reference, a safe, a drawer, another branch's, an inactive and another shop's bank, another shop's customer, an unknown method, card, a field that does not belong to the method, not a list.
- **Reconciliation:** every stock level, cash balance and store credit balance = its ledger; the payments of every sale add up to its total; `app.ledger_mismatches()` is empty. Grants: the new read for authenticated, not anon; the helpers not granted.
- **Other suites:** `s01_sale_core_test.sql` (new signatures in the grants case), `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial case for the new read), `security_guards_test.sql` (allowlist entry), `ledger_invariants_test.sql` (replay list unchanged: `public.post_sale`, `app.post_sale_core`).
