# R04 Guarantors test plan

**Slice:** R04 · Guarantors (EC-66). **Decision:** D71. **Done when:** editing a customer never deletes their guarantors; a contract that needs a guarantor can't be created without one.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/r04_guarantors_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/r04_guarantors_test.sql
```

- **Create:** trims, joins spaces, normalises the phone and the ID (Arabic digits become 0-9); birth date and gender read from the ID; a customer with the same ID shows as "also a customer"; optional fields default to null. Refused with nothing written: no name, phone or national ID; an empty or null ID; century 4, 30 February, 13 digits, a future birth date; a bad phone or a list of phones; a one-letter or 151-letter name; address over 300, job over 100; a negative, three-decimal or numeric income; `organization_id`, `customer_id`, `archived_at` and `birth_date` in the body.
- **Done when (duplicates):** the same national ID written with dashes and Arabic digits → `GUARANTOR_NATIONAL_ID_TAKEN`, the details name the guarantor who has it; the same phone on another guarantor is allowed; the ID never appears in `audit_log`, only its mask.
- **Create with a link:** the guarantor, the link and two audit rows come out of one call; a customer that doesn't exist (or is another shop's), a bad relation, a missing relation, an extra key, a link that is not an object, a customer id that is not a uuid → nothing is created.
- **Update:** changed keys only in the audit; the same values again (written differently) write nothing; `''` and null clear the optional fields; the phone, ID and name can't be cleared; an ID another guarantor has is refused with its details; a changed ID is a `warning` with the two masks and `national_id_changed`, never the number, and moves the "also a customer" link; without `customer.view_pii` the ID can't be sent at all, whatever the value, while the other fields can.
- **Masking:** a cashier gets no ID, birth date or gender back (create, read, list); finds a guarantor by the whole ID but not by a prefix; the owner gets all of it. Search by name (letter case, Arabic letter forms), phone however it is written, order and paging.
- **Links:** link, same pair again (`GUARANTOR_ALREADY_LINKED`), a sixth (`GUARANTOR_LIMIT_REACHED`, nothing written), the customer as their own guarantor (same ID, made later or linked by `customer_id`: `GUARANTOR_IS_CUSTOMER`), an archived guarantor, a blocked customer (`CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`), a bad relation; relation change audited with old and new, the same relation writes nothing; unlink audited as a warning, the guarantor stays, the slot is free again.
- **Done when (customer edit):** a customer with five guarantors is edited through `update_customer` (name, phones, ID fill) and blocked and unblocked → exactly the same five links and relations.
- **Archive / restore:** archive, readable and listed under `archived`, no new link, restore, the same state again writes no audit row.
- **Settings:** `guarantors_required_count` 1-3 (0, 4, text, 1.5 and null → `POLICY_INVALID`); older versions read 1; a new version keeps the count when the key is not sent; the same terms again publish no version; the column is a non-null smallint with a check.
- **Done when (contracts)** (contracts seeded as the database owner; policy: above 10 000.00 needs 2): `app.guarantors_required` 10 000.00 → 0, 10 000.01 → 2, another shop's policy or an unknown one → 0, a policy amount of NULL → 0; `app.check_contract_guarantors` passes with 2 or 3 linked active guarantors and with none when nothing is required, and refuses one (`GUARANTOR_REQUIRED` with `{required: 2, given: 1}`), one of another customer or not linked (`GUARANTOR_NOT_LINKED`), another shop's (`GUARANTOR_NOT_FOUND`), an archived one, duplicates, four ids; **the trigger:** 15 000.00 with none, or with one of two, fails at commit with `GUARANTOR_REQUIRED` and leaves nothing behind; with two attached in the same transaction it commits; 8 000.00 and exactly 10 000.00 with none commit; an `is_migrated` contract with none commits; `contract_guarantors` rows can't be updated or deleted; `app.attach_contract_guarantors` inserts, refuses duplicates, four, another shop's and archived ones.
- **Active contracts:** the customer's list flags `on_active_contract`; unlink and archive are refused while the guarantor is on an active contract, allowed after a completed one; the details answer the customers and the contracts.
- **Requirement read:** required count, linked active count and `met` against the current policy; at the limit nothing is needed; an archived guarantor doesn't count; bad, negative or missing amounts; another shop's customer.
- **Who, plan, expired shop, tenancy, grants:** a cashier (`customer.create`) creates and links but can't edit, relate, unlink, archive or restore; a collector reads masked and can't write; a warehouse user can't read; not an employee; a Starter shop → `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN` on all ten; an expired shop reads but can't write; shop B gets not found everywhere (also as the `guarantor_id` of a link or the customer of one), sees only its own, and may register the same national ID; no direct table writes for `authenticated`; `national_id` has no column grant; the ten functions are granted to `authenticated` only, the helpers to nobody.
- **ID photos:** `register_file` takes the entity type `guarantor` (purpose `customer_id`, key `customer.create`): a cashier registers one, the count shows for everyone, another shop's or an unknown guarantor is `NOT_FOUND`, a collector is refused.
- **Other suites:** `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases for the ten functions), `security_guards_test.sql` (the four reads allowlisted).

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/guarantors.test.ts`: create (required fields, cleaning, bad IDs, phone, income, the link, forbidden keys); update (at least one key, no link, clearing rules); relations and limits; params and queries; the guarantor, list, details, customer list and requirement shapes; the error details and codes; `guarantors_required_count` 1-3 on the policy.
- `src/services/guarantors.service.test.ts`: RPC arguments, paging, create with a link, archive and restore, the four customer calls, the requirement, the duplicate-ID details passed on (and a malformed one dropped), `GUARANTOR_REQUIRED` details, the error mapping. `files.rules.test.ts`: the `guarantor` entity type.
- `src/routes/guarantors.integration.test.ts` (local stack): the owner creates a customer, a guarantor with a link and a second one linked afterwards, edits the customer (`PATCH /api/customers/:id`) and reads the same two; a duplicate ID is 409 with details; a guarantor ID photo is uploaded and counted; a cashier creates and links (masked) but gets 403 on unlink, relation, edit, archive and restore; a collector only reads; archive hides, restore shows; the requirement follows a published policy.
- Cross-shop (212 routes): the 11 routes refuse a foreign header and an org id; shop B's guarantor and customer are not found, not listed (by name or by ID), as a link target or as the customer of a link, and stay unchanged; shop B's national ID is free in shop A; a link to shop B's customer on create leaves no guarantor behind. Audit drivers for the 7 writes.
