# S01 Sale core test plan

**Slice:** S01 · Sale core (EC-68). **Decision:** D62. **Done when:** the four ledger/reconciliation checks pass; double-submit creates one sale; the last unit can't be sold twice.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/s01_sale_core_test.sql`, 85 cases)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/s01_sale_core_test.sql
```

- **Setup:** a shop with two branches; 10 chairs (cost 100) and 3 IMEI phones (cost 3 000, 12 months of warranty) bought through a real purchase invoice; a tablet unit, a phone in the second branch, a free gift and an archived product; a cashier, a second cashier, a manager, an accountant and a warehouse user; customers (one archived, one of another shop).
- **No shift:** no sale and no quote (`SHIFT_REQUIRED`).
- **Quote:** the server's prices, VAT inside the price at 14%, totals, `available` per line; no cost, tax class or warranty fields in the answer; more than is in stock is reported, not refused; nothing written.
- **A sale:** numbered SL in the shift's branch; totals and the tax snapshot as quoted; the phone's warranty date; one cash payment (PY) into the shift's drawer with its allocation; stock down by 2 and 1 and the unit `sold`; the drawer and the shift's expected cash up by the total; one `sale_out` row per line at its cost linked to the line; cost snapshot; audited once.
- **Done when (double submit):** the same key again answers the same sale, even with another cart behind it; documents, ledger rows, numbers and audit rows are counted before and after and don't change.
- **Prices:** another total → `PRICE_CHANGED` with both totals; a price changed after the quote is caught the same way; a cashier's own price → `PRICE_OVERRIDE_DENIED` (also in a quote); the list price sent back is not an override; a manager sells at 200.00 with the tax of 200.00, audited as a warning with both prices.
- **VAT on top of the price:** subtotal 5 250.00 + tax 735.00 = 5 985.00, the mode kept on the sale after the setting changes back; the customer is named.
- **Done when (last unit):** 6 chairs when 5 remain → `OUT_OF_STOCK` with the product, the 6 and the 5; the sold IMEI again → `UNIT_NOT_AVAILABLE`; after the last 5 chairs the next sale is refused, the level is exactly 0 and refused sales took no number; a unit reserved for an order is not sold to a walk-in.
- **Refused, nothing left behind:** an empty cart, not a list, quantity 0 or 1.5, an unknown field, a bad id, a price with three decimals or negative, 101 lines, a product twice, a unit twice, a unit with quantity 2, a total of 0.00, a total that is not money, a `system:` or short key; an unknown or archived product; a serialized product without its unit, a unit on a plain product, a unit of another product, an unknown unit, a unit in the other branch; another shop's product and customer, an archived customer. The footprint (sales, lines, stock rows, payments, allocations, cash rows, counters, audit rows), the levels and the drawer are the same before and after.
- **Who:** a cashier without a shift can't sell; a cashier reads their own sale and not another's; a WAREHOUSE user can't sell, quote or read; an ACCOUNTANT can't sell but reads every sale of the branch.
- **Tenancy, expired shop, closed shift, closed day:** shop B gets `SALE_NOT_FOUND`; no direct writes to `sales` or `payments`; an expired shop quotes and reads but can't sell; after the cashier closes the shift there is no shift to sell in; a closed day refuses a sale.
- **Done when (four checks):** every stock level = its ledger sum and none is below zero; every cash balance = its ledger sum; `app.ledger_mismatches()` is empty; the sales' totals = their payments = the drawers' sale movements; the safe received the closed shift's cash. Grants: the three public functions only.
- **Other suites:** `ledger_invariants_test.sql` (replay list: `post_sale`, `app.post_sale_core`), `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases: a second shift, quote, sale, read), `security_guards_test.sql` (the two reads allowlisted).

## Concurrency (`scripts/db-stock-cash-proof.mjs`, `npm run db:stock-cash`, a step of the CI db job)

Four cashiers, each in their own shift, 20 concurrent sessions calling the real `post_sale`:

- **F.** 20 sales of the last chair → exactly 1 sells, 19 `OUT_OF_STOCK`, one number taken.
- **G.** 20 sales of one IMEI unit → exactly 1; the unit is sold by one `sale_out` row.
- **H.** 20 requests with one key → one sale, one stock movement, one cash movement, one payment, one number.
- **I.** 20 two-product carts, half chair-then-lamp and half lamp-then-chair, for 10 of each → exactly 10 sell, no deadlock.
- The sales' totals = payments = drawer movements; the shop and the whole database reconcile.
