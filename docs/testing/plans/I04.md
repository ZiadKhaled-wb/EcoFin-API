# I04 Serial/IMEI units test plan

**Slice:** I04 · Serial/IMEI units (EC-50). **Decision:** D50. **Done when:** a duplicate serial is rejected; you can't sell a unit that's not in stock in that branch; a unit's history shows every step.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/i04_units_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/i04_units_test.sql
```

- **Register:** three serials (trimmed, upper-cased) in stock in main at their cost, one `opening` movement each from a completed `opening_stock` import job; the same key returns the same batch.
- **Done when (duplicates):** a serial already in the shop (any letter case) → `SERIAL_TAKEN` with the serial in the detail and nothing of the batch created; a serial twice in one batch → `SERIAL_TAKEN`.
- **Refusals:** an unusable serial (`SERIAL_INVALID`), no serials or a bad cost (`VALIDATION_FAILED`), a product without serials (`UNIT_NOT_ALLOWED`), an archived or another shop's product (`PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`), another shop's branch, a `system:` key, a branch the keeper doesn't work in (`BRANCH_DENIED`), a cashier (`PERMISSION_DENIED`), a plan without serials (`MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN`), an expired shop (`SUBSCRIPTION_EXPIRED`); audit rows with the serials.
- **Done when (no sale of a unit that isn't here):** selling a unit of second in main → `UNIT_NOT_AVAILABLE`; a sold unit can't be sold again.
- **Done when (history):** a unit registered, transferred (I03) and written off (I02) shows all four steps with branches, the transfer number and route, the adjustment number and reason; a sold unit shows the sale number and the customer.
- **Security review:** a unit that started in second and came to main: a cashier of main sees second's steps without document, party or employee, and main's step in full.
- **Reads:** find by serial (any case, spaces trimmed), unknown serial, search (prefix, status, branch, paging), a cashier sees only main's units, shop B finds nothing of shop A; the I01 drill-down names transfers; ledger caches match; grants.
- **Guard suites:** `security_guards_test.sql` allowlists the three reads; `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` has a denial case for all four functions (a serialized product of shop one made in the setup); `ledger_invariants_test.sql` lists the register replay.

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/units.test.ts`: serials trimmed and upper-cased, repeats and bad serials refused, the unit with its history, the queries.
- `src/services/units.service.test.ts`: RPC arguments, `SERIAL_TAKEN` details kept only when valid, find by serial or id, search filters.
- `src/routes/units.integration.test.ts` (local stack, seed shop one): register (no key → 400, repeat key → same batch), a duplicate refused with the serial named, a transfer, the unit found by its serial in any case with its three steps and the transfer number, the search.
- Cross-shop cases for the four routes (route count 93); the audit driver for `POST /api/units`; permission markers.
