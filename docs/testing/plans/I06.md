# I06 Low-stock alerts test plan

**Slice:** I06 · Low-stock alerts (EC-52). **Decision:** D52. **Done when:** a product below its min level appears in the list and on the dashboard.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/i06_low_stock_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/i06_low_stock_test.sql
```

- **Levels:** with no override, the product's level (cable 10) or the shop's (tv 3); an override wins in its branch only; 0 means never low there; setting again updates the one row; NULL falls back to the product's level; `list_branch_min_levels` lists each branch's level and source.
- **Done when:** every low product and branch is in `list_stock(p_low_only)` and in `low_stock_summary`, the two agree on the count, and a product back above its level leaves both.
- **Dashboard:** low count per active branch (main first), the biggest shortages first, the limit.
- **Never low:** an archived product (still listed while it holds stock) and any product in a deactivated branch (not on the dashboard).
- **Refusals:** a level below 0 or above 1 000 000, another shop's product or branch, an inactive branch, a dashboard limit above 20; a branch manager in another branch (`BRANCH_DENIED`), a cashier without catalog.manage, a collector without stock.view, not an employee; an expired shop can't change levels but still reads.
- **Audit:** one `stock.min_level_set` row per change, with the branch, before and after (a removal records null).
- **Tenancy:** shop B sees none of shop A's overrides through RLS; no direct writes to the table; grants.
- **Guard suites:** `security_guards_test.sql` allowlists the two reads; `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` has a denial case for all three functions. No idempotency key (a level is set, not posted). `i01_stock_view_test.sql`: an archived product is no longer in the low list.

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/stock.test.ts`: `min_source` on stock rows, the level body (0-1 000 000 or null, strict), the summary query (1-20, default 5) and result.
- `src/services/stock.service.test.ts`: RPC arguments for the summary and the levels; a null level leaves the argument out; `BRANCH_INACTIVE` mapped.
- `src/routes/stock.integration.test.ts` (local stack): a branch level above the stock makes the product low in the list and on the card; removing it falls back to the product's level and the product leaves both; a bad level → 400; a collector → 403 on all three routes.
- Cross-shop cases (97 routes): the summary never shows shop B and refuses its header and an org key; the levels refuse shop B's product; setting refuses shop B's product (no audit rows) and branch, and an `organization_id` in the body. Audit driver for `PUT /api/stock/min-levels/:id`; permission markers.
