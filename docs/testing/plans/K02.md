# K02 Shifts / cash drawer test plan

**Slice:** K02 · Shifts / cash drawer (EC-60). **Decision:** D60. **Done when:** expected cash = float + drawer ledger sum (not the whole vault); a closed shift can't take sales.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/k02_shifts_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/k02_shifts_test.sql
```

- **Setup:** the main safe holds 5 000.00; two cashiers, an accountant and a warehouse user in main; a second branch; POS devices (one in main, one in the second branch, one inactive).
- **Open:** a cashier opens with a float of 500.00 from the safe → an open shift numbered SH, its own drawer account, expected 500.00, the safe 4 500.00, one `float_in` movement with a CX number, audited once; the same key → the same shift, nothing moves; a second open → `SHIFT_ALREADY_OPEN`.
- **Done when (expected cash):** with a cash sale of 300.00 and a refund of 50.00 on the drawer (as S01 will post them), expected = 750.00 while the safe holds 4 500.00; the baseline view agrees.
- **Cash in and out:** 200.00 dropped into the safe (the same key → nothing moves, one audit row); 100.00 of change taken; refused: an unknown direction, no note, amount 0, another branch's safe, a bank, another shop's safe, an inactive safe, more out than the drawer holds, more in than the safe holds, a `system:` key; nothing changes.
- **Close:** an unknown denomination and a fractional count → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; counted 640.00 of 650.00 without a reason → `SHIFT_DIFFERENCE_REASON_REQUIRED` with the three amounts; counted cash without a safe, or into another branch's safe, refused; with a reason → closed, expected / counted / difference / breakdown kept, the drawer at 0, the counted cash in the safe, one `adjustment` of −10.00, audited as a warning; closing again → unchanged.
- **Done when (closed shift):** a cash movement tagged with the closed shift → `SHIFT_CLOSED`; `app.require_open_shift` → `SHIFT_REQUIRED`; no cash moves on a closed shift; no current shift.
- **A second shift:** a float needs a safe and no float takes none; a negative float, more than the safe holds, a device of another branch, an inactive device and a branch the cashier doesn't work in are refused; the same drawer is reused, on a device, expected 0.00 (nothing carried over); the device can't take a second open shift; another cashier gets their own drawer; an exact count of 0 needs no reason and no safe.
- **Who:** a cashier can't read, close or move cash on another cashier's shift and lists only their own; the vault list shows the branch's active safes by name without balances; the owner adds cash to and closes a cashier's shift (an overage of 5.00); an ACCOUNTANT reads every shift but can't open one; a WAREHOUSE user can't read or open.
- **List:** filters by status, cashier, branch and day; paging; an unknown status and cashier refused. The safe's K01 transfer list shows the float, the moves and the closings; the safe adds up.
- **Tenancy, ledger, expired shop, grants:** shop B gets not found for a's shift, branch, safe and vault list; no direct writes to `shifts`; `app.ledger_mismatches()` is empty; an expired shop reads but can't open; the seven functions are granted, the helpers aren't.
- **Other suites:** `ledger_invariants_test.sql` (replay list: `open_shift`, `move_shift_cash`), `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases for the seven functions), `security_guards_test.sql` (the four reads allowlisted); `k01_vaults_test.sql`, `smoke_test.sql` and `f10_ledger_test.sql` pass with the new trigger.

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/shifts.test.ts`: open (a float needs a safe, no float takes none, strict), cash (direction, amount above 0, note), close (Egyptian denominations, whole counts, the exact total), the list query, the current shift, the details and the error codes.
- `src/services/shifts.service.test.ts`: RPC arguments (the safe, device and reason only when given), `SHIFT_DIFFERENCE_REASON_REQUIRED` mapped with its details, no open shift reads as null, the list filters, the vaults.
- `src/routes/shifts.integration.test.ts` (local stack): a cashier sees the safes by name and gets 403 on K01's accounts; a missing key and a float without a safe are 400; **two parallel opens with one key → one shift**; a second open → 409; a drop of 200.00 and change of 100.00 → expected 400.00 with three CX movements; more out than the till holds → `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`; closing 10.00 short without a reason → 400 with the amounts, with a reason → closed; the safe = 5 000 − 500 + 200 − 100 + 390; closing again changes nothing; cash on a closed shift → `SHIFT_CLOSED`; the owner lists and reads the shift.
- Cross-shop (146 routes): the 7 routes refuse a foreign header and an org id; shop B's branch, cashier, safe and shift are refused and B's shift stays open. Audit drivers for the 3 writes (`shift.opened`, `shift.cash_moved`, `shift.closed`).
