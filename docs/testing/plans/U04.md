# U04 Purchase returns test plan

**Slice:** U04 · Purchase returns (EC-56). **Decision:** D57. **Done when:** stock and supplier balance each change exactly once; you can't return more than you bought.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/u04_purchase_returns_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/u04_purchase_returns_test.sql
```

- **Setup:** an approved invoice in main with 10 TVs at 100.00 (14%) and 3 serials of a phone at 5 000.00.
- **Done when (once):** a credit return of 4 TVs + 1 phone serial → stock down by exactly 4 and 1, the unit `returned`, the supplier balance down by the total including VAT, the U01 statement shows the return, one audit row; the same key again → the same return, stock and balance unchanged.
- **Done when (cap):** 7 more TVs with 6 left → `RETURN_QTY_EXCEEDED` with `returnable: 6`, nothing posted; 6 → posted; 1 more → `RETURN_QTY_EXCEEDED` with 0.
- **Tax:** the returns of a line add back exactly to the line's tax and gross (`app.return_line_tax`, D41).
- **Average cost:** 10 on hand at 115.0000, returning 2 at 130.00 → 111.2500; returning everything keeps the last average; a clamp case at 0.
- **Cash refund:** into the main safe → `cash_balances` up by the total, one `supplier_refund` movement, the supplier balance unchanged; an inactive account (`CASH_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE`), another branch's safe, a drawer or another shop's account (`VAULT_INVALID`) refused; cash without an account or credit with one → `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
- **Serial rules:** a unit of another invoice line, a unit already returned, a reserved unit, a unit moved to another branch → `UNIT_NOT_AVAILABLE`; an unknown unit → `UNIT_NOT_FOUND`; nothing posted.
- **Lines:** a line twice, a line of another invoice, quantity on a serialized line or unit_ids on a normal one, an empty list → `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
- **States and roles:** a draft or rejected invoice → `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_APPROVED`; a missing or short reason → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; a WAREHOUSE user → `PERMISSION_DENIED`; another branch's manager → `BRANCH_DENIED`; an expired shop can read but can't post.
- **Reads:** the returnable lines (purchased / returned / returnable, units in stock); the accounts read lists the branch's active safes and banks plus shop-wide banks with balances and is refused without both keys; shop B gets not found for a's returns, invoice and branch.
- **Ledger:** `app.ledger_mismatches()` is empty; the movement history shows the PR number and the supplier.
- **Other suites:** `ledger_invariants_test.sql` (replay list: `post_purchase_return`), `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases for the five functions), `security_guards_test.sql` (the four reads allowlisted).

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/purchaseReturns.test.ts`: a line takes quantity or unit_ids (exactly one, units once), the body (each line once, cash needs an account, credit takes none, reason 3-500, strict), the list query (date order, strict), the returnable invoice, the accounts list, the error codes.
- `src/services/purchases.service.test.ts` (U04 block): RPC arguments (quantity or unit_ids per line, the account only for cash, filters and paging), `RETURN_QTY_EXCEEDED` mapped with its details.
- `src/routes/purchaseReturns.integration.test.ts` (local stack): the owner approves an invoice (10 TVs, 3 phones), reads the returnable lines, posts a credit return (a missing key is 400; the same key → the same body), stock 6 and 2 and the supplier balance down once; 7 more TVs → 409 with `returnable: 6`; a cash return into the main safe raises the safe by the total and leaves the supplier balance; list by invoice and read by id; a warehouse user gets 403 on the post, the returnable lines and the accounts.
- Cross-shop (127 routes): the 5 routes refuse a foreign header and an org id; shop B's return, invoice, invoice line, supplier, branch and cash account are refused and nothing is posted. Audit driver for `POST /api/purchase-returns` (`purchase_return.posted`).
