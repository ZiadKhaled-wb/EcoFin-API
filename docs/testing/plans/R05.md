# R05 Field surveys test plan

**Slice:** R05 · Field surveys (EC-67). **Decision:** D75. **Done when:** photos are private (F14); a required survey blocks the installment sale until approved.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/r05_field_surveys_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/r05_field_surveys_test.sql
```

Shops a and b (retail) and c (starter); in shop a an owner, a cashier, a branch manager, two collectors, a warehouse user and an inactive collector. Decided surveys, aged ones and contracts are seeded as the database owner (nothing writes contracts yet: N02).

- **Schema:** the seven new columns, the visit type and score checks, no deletes.
- **Assign:** to a collector with the visit type, due date and trimmed instructions; a second open survey (assigned or submitted) → `SURVEY_ALREADY_OPEN` with the open one in the details; a cashier, an inactive employee or shop B's employee → `SURVEY_ASSIGNEE_INVALID` (details: the employee); a blocked or archived customer → `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`; shop B's or an unknown customer → `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`; a past due date, an unknown key, a bad visit type, instructions over 500, no assignee → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; a watch customer can be surveyed.
- **Reads:** the owner sees all with the submitted ones first; filters by status, customer, assignee and mine; a collector sees only their own (another's → `SURVEY_NOT_FOUND`, the customer list too).
- **Reassign and cancel:** the same assignee is no change; to a cashier → `SURVEY_ASSIGNEE_INVALID`; a submitted survey can't be reassigned; cancel needs a reason (3-300), works from assigned, refused when cancelled or approved.
- **Fill:** partial scores and checklist kept, one audit row with the changed keys, the same values again write nothing; null removes a key; scores 0, 11, 7.5 or text, unknown criteria or items, a non-boolean answer, half a GPS fix, latitude 91, a capture time an hour ahead or before the survey, notes over 2000, an unknown key, an empty body → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; the manager (has survey.submit) on another's survey → `SURVEY_NOT_ASSIGNEE`; another collector → `SURVEY_NOT_FOUND`.
- **Photos (Done when):** the assignee's upload sits in the private bucket; survey_photo without its survey, another purpose on a survey, another assignee's survey, an 11th photo, a submitted survey → refused; shop B can't attach a photo to shop A's survey.
- **Submit:** missing criteria → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; complete answers without a photo → `SURVEY_PHOTO_REQUIRED`; criteria 8,7,6,9,5,7 → score 70, 150 m flagged as low accuracy; again, a save or a photo after it → `SURVEY_WRONG_STATUS`.
- **Decide:** a rejection without a note, an unknown decision → `VALIDATION_FAILED`; an assigned survey → `SURVEY_WRONG_STATUS`; the manager approves (valid for 180 days); twice → `SURVEY_WRONG_STATUS`; the manager on their own survey → `SURVEY_SELF_DECISION`, the owner may; a decided survey can't change even as the database owner (`DOCUMENT_FINAL`).
- **Credit:** residence 0 before the approval, 0.70 × weight 20 = 14 points after; the profile's `latest_survey`; an approval 181 days old and a rejection count 0.
- **Contract rule (Done when):** `app.survey_required` 20 000.00 → no, 20 000.01 → yes, no amount → never, another shop's policy → never; `app.check_contract_survey` without a survey, with a submitted one (named as open), with a rejected one (last decision), with an old approval → `SURVEY_REQUIRED` with the details; a valid approval is returned; another customer's doesn't count. The commit-time trigger refuses a 25 000 contract without an approved survey and removes it, commits one with a valid survey, one of 15 000 and a migrated one.
- **Requirement read:** required and met with the survey, open but not met with a submitted one, met below the amount; a bad amount, shop B's customer → refused.
- **Who:** a cashier reads but can't assign, save, decide or cancel; a warehouse user can't read; not an employee → `NOT_AN_EMPLOYEE`; a starter shop → `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN`; an expired shop reads but can't write (`SUBSCRIPTION_EXPIRED`).
- **Tenancy and grants:** shop B sees none of shop A's surveys and gets not found everywhere; no direct table writes; exactly the ten public functions are granted; no app helper of R05 is.
- **Audit:** one row per action, entity type `field_survey`; the cancel is a warning with its reason; the submit holds the score; no row holds the GPS point; refused calls wrote nothing.
- **Other suites:** `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial cases for the ten functions; the `register_file(survey_photo)` case now names its survey), `security_guards_test.sql` (the four reads allowlisted).

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/surveys.test.ts`: the six criteria and items, a survey with and without answers, scores outside 1-10, decimals and unknown keys refused, nulls in a save, an empty submit, GPS ranges, notes, the assign, cancel and decision bodies, the `mine` flag, the requirement, the profile's `latest_survey`, `SURVEY_REQUIRED` details and the statuses.
- `src/services/surveys.service.test.ts`: RPC names and arguments (filters left out when absent, the note only when there is one), answers parsed, `SURVEY_ALREADY_OPEN` 409 with details, `SURVEY_NOT_ASSIGNEE` 403, `SURVEY_NOT_FOUND` 404, no body in the log, an unknown error 500.
- `src/routes/surveys.integration.test.ts` (local stack): the owner assigns to a collector; a second survey → 409; the collector sees it in Mine, another collector gets 404 (and 403 on a photo); the collector saves, gets `SURVEY_PHOTO_REQUIRED`, uploads a photo (not listed for the collector, listed for the owner), submits (score 70, low accuracy); the collector and a cashier get 403 on decide, the manager rejects without a note → 400 and approves; twice → 409; the credit profile shows `latest_survey` and the residence points; the customer list for a cashier; reassign to a cashier → 409, to the second collector; the first collector then gets 404; a cashier can't assign; cancel needs a reason; the requirement follows the published policy and names the open survey.
- Audit drivers for the six writes; cross-shop (234 routes): every route refuses shop B's survey or customer, a foreign header and a tenant key; assigning shop B's employee → `SURVEY_ASSIGNEE_INVALID`.
