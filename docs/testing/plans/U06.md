# U06 Purchase approval by amount test plan

**Slice:** U06 · Purchase approval by amount (EC-58). **Decision:** D55. **Done when:** a user below the threshold can't approve a large invoice; the approval list shows waiting invoices.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/u06_purchase_approval_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/u06_purchase_approval_test.sql
```

Totals use exempt lines, so the total equals the cost. Threshold 10 000.00 unless said otherwise.

- **No threshold (NULL):** a BRANCH_MANAGER approves a 50 000 invoice directly (stock in); the audit records `threshold: null, above: false`.
- **Exactly 10 000.00:** approved directly (strictly greater).
- **Done when:** a BRANCH_MANAGER approving 12 000 gets `pending_approval` with its approval (amount, threshold, requester) — no stock movement, no `avg_cost` change, no supplier balance change, one `pending` approval row, one `approval_requested` audit row; approving again → `APPROVAL_OWNER_ONLY`; editing it → `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_EDITABLE`; rejecting it as a manager → `APPROVAL_OWNER_ONLY`.
- **Done when (list):** the manager of main and the owner see it, with the invoice summary; the manager of another branch doesn't (and can't filter by main: `BRANCH_DENIED`); a WAREHOUSE user gets `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
- **Owner, pending:** no note or a note under 3 → `APPROVAL_NOTE_REQUIRED`, nothing posted; with a note → stock in once, cost updated (50 000 + 12 000 → 31 000.0000), balance up, the approval `approved` by the owner with the note (requested by the manager), the audit `reason` = the note; approving again → unchanged; it moves from the pending to the approved list.
- **Owner rejects another pending one:** the invoice and the approval are `rejected` with the reason, by the owner; nothing posted; audited.
- **Owner, draft above the threshold:** approved directly, no approval row; the audit has `above: true`.
- **Pending stays pending:** with the threshold removed, a manager's approve of a pending invoice is still `APPROVAL_OWNER_ONLY`, while a new 90 000 draft posts directly.
- **Constraints:** a second pending approval for the same invoice is refused by the index; a decided approval is frozen (`DOCUMENT_FINAL`).
- **Tenancy, expired shop, grants:** shop B can't approve or reject (not found) and lists nothing; an expired shop reads the list but can't decide; an unknown status is refused; ledger caches match; the new `approve_purchase_invoice(uuid, text)` and `list_purchase_approvals` are granted, the old one-argument signature is gone, helpers are not granted.
- **Other suites:** `u02_purchase_invoices_test.sql` (grant checks use the new signature, 93 passing unchanged), `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` (denial for `list_purchase_approvals`), `security_guards_test.sql` (allowlist).
