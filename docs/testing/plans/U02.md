# U02 Purchase invoices test plan

**Slice:** U02 · Purchase invoices (EC-54). **Decision:** D54. **Done when:** approving a multi-line invoice adds each quantity exactly once; cost updates correctly; the supplier balance increases by the total.

## SQL (`supabase/tests/u02_purchase_invoices_test.sql`)

```text
npm run db:smoke -- supabase/tests/u02_purchase_invoices_test.sql
```

- **Draft:** numbered PI; 14% + exempt + 14% lines give subtotal 3 322.00, tax 434.00, total 3 756.00; per-line net, tax, rate and total; serials trimmed and upper-cased; no units, stock, cost or balance before approval; one `purchase_invoice.created` row.
- **Idempotency:** the same create key returns the same invoice (one row, one audit); a `system:` key is refused.
- **Tax:** net 1 000.00 at 14% → 140.00; a class rate changed between draft and approval (10% → 5%) is applied at approval, and the supplier balance rises by the recomputed total.
- **Done when (quantities):** the 3-line invoice (2 normal lines, 1 serialized with 2 serials) raises each stock level by exactly the line quantity; one `purchase_in` row per normal line and per serial, at the net unit cost; approving again changes nothing (ledger, stock, cost, audit).
- **Done when (cost):** 10 at 100.0000 + 10 at 130.00 → 115.0000 (stock in two branches, one unit damaged, all counted); no stock, buy at 55.50 → 55.5000; 3 at 10.00 + 1 at 11.00 → 10.2500; VAT never enters the average; the approval audit keeps the cost before and after.
- **Done when (balance):** `v_supplier_balances` rises by the total including VAT; U01's statement lists the invoice.
- **Serials:** the units are in stock in the branch at the unit cost and linked to the line; `find_unit` and the movement history (I05) show the PI number and the supplier; a serial already in the shop, repeated across lines, not matching the quantity, invalid, or on a non-serialized product is refused; approval without serials → `SERIALS_REQUIRED` with the line id and nothing posted; a serial taken after the draft → `SERIAL_TAKEN`, nothing posted, still a draft.
- **Update:** one audit row with what changed; no change, no row; lines replaced and totals recomputed; a future date, an unknown key (the supplier is fixed) and an approved invoice are refused.
- **Reject:** reason 3-500, audited as a warning; a rejected invoice can't be edited, approved or rejected again, frees its supplier invoice number, and is frozen by the trigger; an approved invoice can't be rejected; a duplicate supplier invoice number on a live invoice → `SUPPLIER_INVOICE_NO_TAKEN`.
- **Refusals:** archived supplier (create, and approve after the draft) → `SUPPLIER_ARCHIVED`; another shop's supplier, branch, product or tax class; an archived product; a future date; a product twice; quantity 0; a cost with 3 decimals or negative; no lines; an unknown line key; a supplier invoice number over 40; a serialized line in a shop without the serials module.
- **Roles and tenancy:** a WAREHOUSE user creates and edits but can't approve or reject; a BRANCH_MANAGER of main approves main's invoices but can't approve, create, read or filter in another branch; a cashier can't read or create; not an employee; an expired shop reads but can't write; shop B gets `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` everywhere and sees nothing.
- **List:** status, supplier, branch and search filters; summaries carry `lines_count`, not lines; `from > to` and an unknown status are refused.
- **Ledger and grants:** `app.ledger_mismatches()` is empty; no anon grants; helpers not granted.
- **Guard suites:** `security_guards_test.sql` allowlists the two reads; `tenant_and_permission_test.sql` has denial cases for all six functions; `ledger_invariants_test.sql` lists `create_purchase_invoice` as replay-tested.

## Contracts and API

- `packages/contracts/src/purchases.test.ts`: create body (trimmed number, upper-cased serials, every refused line), update (supplier and branch fixed), reject reason, invoice and list shapes, list query, error codes and the `SERIALS_REQUIRED` detail.
- `src/services/purchases.service.test.ts`: RPC arguments for every call (filters left out when not given, optional line keys left out), `SERIALS_REQUIRED` details mapped, raw messages never leak.
- `src/routes/purchaseInvoices.integration.test.ts` (local stack): an owner creates a 2-line draft (one serialized), the same key returns it, approval without serials → 422 with the line id, edit adds the serials, approve twice (same result), `GET /api/stock` shows the quantities, the supplier balance is up; a warehouse user creates and edits but gets 403 on approve and reject; a missing key → 400, a duplicate supplier invoice number → 409, a rejected invoice can't be edited.
- Cross-shop cases (110 routes): the list refuses a foreign header, an org key and shop B's supplier; read, edit, approve and reject answer `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` for shop B's invoice and leave it unchanged; create refuses a foreign header, an `organization_id` in the body and shop B's supplier, branch or product; no audit rows in either shop. Audit drivers for the 4 writes; permission markers.
