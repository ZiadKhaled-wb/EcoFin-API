## Goal
When an installment sale is made, the cashier prints, on A4, in Arabic: **the contract** (the shop, the customer, the guarantors, the goods, the installment price and how it is paid, the full schedule, the owner's terms text, signature blocks) and **the promissory notes** (كمبيالات) the customer and the guarantors sign, one per installment or one for the total, as the shop chose. Every amount and date on the paper comes from the server's answer for that contract, so the paper matches the contract exactly. The owner edits the terms text in settings; the system refuses wording the Islamic finance rules forbid. The first print of each document is the original; every later print says **صورة (COPY)**.

## Decisions (record them in D82)
1. **Drawn by the client, data from one read (D68's pattern).** `GET /api/contracts/:id/documents` → `public.get_contract_documents(p_id)` answers everything the two documents print: N02's contract JSON (current schedule version), the customer and every guarantor **with full national ID, address, job and phone** (they are on the signed paper), the goods (the sale's lines: name, quantity, line total; none for a migrated contract), the shop (`legal_name` or `name`, `commercial_register`, `tax_registration_no`, `address`, `phone`, `logo_file_id`, the branch name and address), the terms text (decision 4) and the promissory notes (decision 5), and the print counters (decision 6). The browser only formats: dates, money with `formatMoney`, and amounts **in words** with the new contracts helper (decision 7). No money arithmetic in the browser.
2. **Who may print:** holders of `installment.create` (the person who makes the sale prints it) or `customer.view_pii`, with the contract visibility rule (`app.can_see_contract`); module `installments`; an expired shop may still read and print (prints are logged only when the shop can write, as S04). Because the answer carries full national IDs, it is **not** cached by the Web beyond the print view, and every print is logged with who printed it (decision 6). Say in D82 that `installment.create` holders see national IDs on this one read (see To confirm 1).
3. **Which contracts:** the contract document prints for any status (a closed contract is printed for the file); a completed, written-off or returned contract prints with its status across the top ("مسدد" / "دين معدوم" / "مرتجع"). Promissory notes print only for an `active` or `rescheduled` contract with something left (else `CONTRACT_NOT_OPEN`, N03's code).
4. **The terms text (owner-editable):** new `org_settings.contract_terms text NULL` (≤ 20 000 characters). `NULL` means the default text, `DEFAULT_CONTRACT_TERMS_AR` in the contracts package (draft it from the old project's clauses if `docs/OLD_CODE_MAP.md` lists any, else write plain clauses: the sale is a deferred-price sale of goods the shop owns; the installment price and schedule are fixed; ownership and delivery; the customer's duty to pay on the dates; the guarantors are jointly liable; disputes go to the courts of the shop's city; **nothing about interest, late fees or discounts**). It is saved through the existing `PATCH /api/settings` (`update_org_settings`), `org.settings_manage`, audit `org.settings_updated` as today. Placeholders `{{name}}` from a fixed list (`shop_name`, `shop_legal_name`, `commercial_register`, `branch_name`, `city`, `contract_number`, `contract_date`, `customer_name`, `customer_national_id`, `installment_price`, `installment_price_words`, `down_payment`, `installments_count`, `frequency`, `first_due_date`, `last_due_date`); an unknown one → `TEMPLATE_INVALID` (422, `{unknown: [...]}`). **Forbidden wording** (rules 3, 4, 6), checked case-insensitively after removing Arabic diacritics: `فائدة`, `فوائد`, `سعر الفائدة`, `قرض`, `غرامة`, `غرامات`, `غرامة تأخير`, `خصم السداد المبكر`, `interest`, `loan`, `late fee`, `penalty` → `TEMPLATE_NOT_ALLOWED` (422, `{words: [...]}`), the same list exported by the contracts package so the settings screen warns while typing. The rendered text substitutes placeholders on the client from the answer's values.
5. **Promissory notes:** new `org_settings.promissory_notes_mode text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'per_installment' CHECK (IN ('per_installment', 'single'))`, saved through `PATCH /api/settings`. The answer's `notes` list is computed by the server from the **current schedule version** (N05), lines with something left only:
   - `per_installment`: one note per such line: `seq`, `amount` = the line's remaining, `due_date` = the line's due date.
   - `single`: one note: `amount` = the sum of those lines' remaining, `due_date` = the **last** of their due dates.
   Every note carries: number `{contract number}-{seq or 'T'}`, the drawer (the customer: name, national ID, address), the beneficiary (the shop's legal name), the place and date of issue (the branch's city, the contract's date or the print date for a reprint after a reschedule, see decision 6), and the guarantors as joint guarantors (ضامن متضامن) with name and national ID. The sum of the notes always equals what is left on the contract (tested). No note ever carries anything but installment money (rule 4).
6. **Print log = audit log (S04 / N03 pattern), per document and schedule version:** `POST /api/contracts/:id/document-prints` body `{ document: 'contract' | 'promissory_notes' }` → `public.record_contract_document_print(p_id, p_document)`: locks the contract, counts earlier `contract.document_printed` rows for this contract, document and **current version**, writes one with `{number, document, version, print_no, copy}`, answers `{ print_no, copy }`. Print 1 of a version is the original; later ones say COPY. After a reschedule (new version) the first print of each document is an original again and says "replaces the schedule / notes of {date of the earlier version}". The documents read answers the next `print_no` per document so the preview shows the mark. No idempotency key (each press is one print, as S04); same readers as decision 2; in an expired shop the call fails and the client prints a COPY (S04's rule).
7. **Amount in words:** contracts helper `amountInWordsAr(money: Money): string` ("ألف ومائتان وخمسون جنيهًا وخمسون قرشًا فقط لا غير"), Egyptian pounds and piasters, correct Arabic number agreement up to 999 999 999 999.99, unit-tested on at least 40 amounts (0.01, 1, 2, 3, 10, 11, 12, 20, 100, 101, 200, 1 000, 1 001, 2 000, 3 000, 11 000, 100 000, 1 000 000, 1 250.50, edge cases). Used for the installment price in the contract and for every note. (An English helper is not needed: both documents are Arabic only.)
8. **Paper and layout (Web):** A4 portrait, Arabic, RTL, the shop logo (S04's loader and its timeout) and header on the contract's first page; page numbers "صفحة n من m"; the schedule table never splits a row across pages; signature blocks for the customer, each guarantor and the shop. Notes: **three per A4 page** with cut lines, each self-contained. A COPY mark across every page of a copy. Printed through the browser (`window.print()`, `@media print`), like S04; no PDF on the server. Saving the signed scans is F14's existing `contract_pdf` / `promissory_note` purposes (out of scope here, see below).
9. **Where to print from:** the contract page ("طباعة العقد", "طباعة الكمبيالات") and the POS installment-sale done card (the same two buttons right after the sale). Each opens a print view `/contracts/:id/print?doc=contract|notes` that loads the documents read, shows the preview with the copy mark, records the print on "Print", then calls `window.print()`.
10. **Settings screen (P03, installments tab):** "Contract and promissory notes": the notes mode (radio), the terms text (textarea, placeholder chips that insert `{{…}}`, live warning for forbidden words and unknown placeholders, "restore the default text", a preview rendered with sample values).

## Routes (2)
1. `GET /api/contracts/:id/documents` → `public.get_contract_documents(p_id)`: decision 1-5. Answer `ContractDocuments` (`contract`, `customer`, `guarantors`, `goods`, `shop`, `terms: { text, is_default }`, `notes_mode`, `notes`, `prints: { contract: { next_print_no, copy }, promissory_notes: { next_print_no, copy } }`, `status_mark`).
2. `POST /api/contracts/:id/document-prints` body `{ document }` (strict) → `public.record_contract_document_print(p_id, p_document)`: decision 6. Answer `{ print_no, copy }`.
Changed: `GET /api/settings` answers `contract_terms` (null = default) and `promissory_notes_mode`; `PATCH /api/settings` accepts them (decisions 4-5).
New errors: `TEMPLATE_INVALID` (422), `TEMPLATE_NOT_ALLOWED` (422). Reused: `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND` (404; another shop's id too), `CONTRACT_NOT_OPEN` (409, notes of a closed contract), `PERMISSION_DENIED`.

## Done when
- **The paper matches the contract exactly:** every schedule line's date and amount, the installment price, down payment, financed amount and profit on the printed contract equal `GET /api/contracts/:id` (Web unit test on the rendered document + SQL test on the read); the notes' amounts sum to the contract's remaining on the current version, per line and in single mode.
- **Rules on paper:** only the installment price is stated (no cash-price alternative beside it), the word profit appears and interest never does, no late fee and no discount promise anywhere; a template with a forbidden word or an unknown placeholder is refused by the server and warned by the screen.
- **Copies:** first print of each document per schedule version is the original, later ones COPY; a reschedule makes the next print an original that says it replaces the earlier one; two clicks in parallel get two different print numbers.
- **Access and isolation:** another shop's contract → `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND`; a caller without `installment.create` or `customer.view_pii` → `PERMISSION_DENIED`; notes of a closed contract → `CONTRACT_NOT_OPEN`.
- All local checks green (unit, contracts, SQL files, integration, Web unit + e2e), as in N05.

## Owner decisions (19 Oct, all defaults confirmed)

2. **Notes mode default:** one promissory note per installment (the owner can switch the shop to a single note).
