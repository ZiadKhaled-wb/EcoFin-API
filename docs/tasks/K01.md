## Goal
The owner and the accountant manage the places the shop keeps money: safes (per branch) and bank accounts (per branch or shop-wide). They see each account's balance, move money between accounts, give a new account its opening balance, and open an account's ledger with a running balance that ends at that balance.

## Slice text (K01)
- **Includes:** create, rename, deactivate; type (cash, bank); branch; balance from the cash ledger; transfer between vaults (out of one, into the other, in one transaction, both vaults in the same shop, amount > 0); vault ledger view.
- **Done when:** a transfer changes both balances once; the ledger view adds up to the balance.

## Decisions (record them in D58)
1. **Kinds managed here:** `safe` (the slice's "cash") and `bank`. Drawers (K02) and collector wallets (N06) are not created, renamed or deactivated here, and are not listed by K01's reads.
2. **Branch:** a safe always has a branch. A bank account may have a branch or be **shop-wide** (`branch_id` NULL, baseline CHECK). The kind and the branch are fixed at creation.
3. **Names** are unique per branch, shop-wide ones among themselves (baseline `cash_accounts_name_key`, NULLS NOT DISTINCT) → `CASH_ACCOUNT_NAME_TAKEN`. Trimmed, 1–100 characters.
4. **Deactivate** (`is_active = false`, `archived_at = now()`) is refused while:
   - the balance is not 0 → `CASH_ACCOUNT_HAS_BALANCE`;
   - it's a branch's default vault (`branches.default_cash_account_id`, O01) → `CASH_ACCOUNT_IS_DEFAULT`.
   Reactivate re-checks the name rule. An inactive account takes no transfers or opening balance (`CASH_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE`) but stays readable.
5. **Transfers** (`cash_transfers`, kind `transfer`, number from `next_document_number(org, branch, 'cash_transfer')`, prefix CX):
   - amount > 0, ≤ 2 decimals; from ≠ to (`TRANSFER_SAME_ACCOUNT`); both active, both the shop's, both `safe` or `bank`;
   - **who:** `cash.transfer`, and the caller needs access to the **source** account's branch (a shop-wide bank: any holder of `cash.transfer`); the destination may be any active safe or bank of the shop;
   - **posting:** one `cash_movements` row out (`transfer_out`, `-amount`) and one in (`transfer_in`, `+amount`), source `cash_transfer`, in one transaction, accounts locked in id order. The ledger trigger refuses a safe going below 0 (`INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, baseline) and a closed day (`DAY_CLOSED`);
   - `cash_movements.branch_id` = the account's branch; for a shop-wide bank, the other account's branch, or the caller's default branch when both are shop-wide banks;
   - **idempotent:** the same `Idempotency-Key` returns the same transfer; `system:` keys are refused;
   - an optional note (`reason`, ≤ 500). Audit `cash_transfer.created`.
   - Transfers are issued documents: never edited or deleted. A reversal is out of scope (K04/V2).
6. **Opening balance (default, confirm with the owner):** an account with **no movements yet** can receive one opening balance (`cash.adjust`): amount > 0, a note; posted as one `cash_movements` row, type `opening`, source `import`, through an `import_jobs` row of kind `opening_cash` (the I04 `register_units` pattern). An account with movements → `CASH_ACCOUNT_HAS_MOVEMENTS`. Idempotent by key. Audit `cash_account.opening_balance`. (Corrections after that are K04's day close or M02's migration, not K01.)
7. **Reads** need `cash.view` (a read: works in an expired shop): the safes and banks of the caller's branches plus every shop-wide bank (OWNER: all). Another shop's or another branch's account → `CASH_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`.
8. **The ledger view** (`GET /api/cash-accounts/:id/movements`): newest first, `from`/`to` business dates; each row carries its running balance after the movement (computed over the account's whole ledger, ties broken by id, as I05 does); the opening balance of the period and the closing balance. With no dates the closing balance equals the account's balance (`cash_balances`).

## Database facts (baseline + O01; don't alter)
- `cash_accounts`: `id, organization_id, branch_id (NULL only for bank), name, kind cash_account_kind (safe, bank, drawer, collector_wallet), employee_id (wallets only), is_active, created_*, updated_*, archived_at`; `cash_accounts_name_key UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT (organization_id, branch_id, name)`; CHECKs `(kind = 'collector_wallet') = (employee_id IS NOT NULL)` and `kind = 'bank' OR branch_id IS NOT NULL`.
- `cash_balances` (trigger-kept cache; **never written by functions**), `v_cash_balances` view. `cash_movements` is append-only; the trigger refuses a safe, drawer or wallet below 0 (`INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`), a bank only unless `org_settings.allow_negative_bank`.
- `cash_movement_type`: … `transfer_in`, `transfer_out`, `opening` …; `doc_type`: `cash_transfer`, `import`; `import_kind`: `opening_cash`.
- `cash_transfers`: `number, from_account_id, to_account_id, amount > 0, kind (transfer), shortage_amount 0, reason, business_date, idempotency_key`; CHECK from ≠ to; append-only.
- P01 provisioning creates each shop's main safe (`الخزنة الرئيسية`); O01's `create_branch` creates a branch safe and sets it as the branch's `default_cash_account_id`; O01's deactivation check sums the branch's cash balances.
- Permissions: `cash.view`, `cash.account_manage`, `cash.transfer`, `cash.adjust` (see the role defaults in `packages/contracts/src/permissions.ts`; OWNER has all).

## Contract (`packages/contracts/src/cash.ts`, new; contracts 0.26.0)
- `CashAccountKind = z.enum(['safe','bank'])`; `CashAccountStatus = z.enum(['active','inactive','all'])`.
- `CashAccount`: `{ id, name, kind, branch_id: Uuid|null, branch_name: string|null, is_active, archived_at, balance: Money, is_default: boolean, created_at, updated_at }`.
- `CashAccountList = z.array(CashAccount)` (a shop has few accounts; no paging). `CashAccountListQuery` (strict): `status` (default `active`), `branch_id?`, `kind?`.
- `CashAccountCreateBody` (strict): `name` (trim 1–100), `kind`, `branch_id: Uuid|null` (required for `safe`; refine).
- `CashAccountUpdateBody` (strict): `{ name }`.
- `CashAccountIdParams`: `{ id: Uuid }`.
- `CashOpeningBody` (strict): `{ amount: Money (> 0), note (trim 3–500) }` + `Idempotency-Key`.
- `CashTransferCreateBody` (strict): `{ from_account_id, to_account_id, amount: Money (> 0), note?: trim ≤ 500 }` (refine from ≠ to) + `Idempotency-Key`.
- `CashTransfer`: `{ id, number, from_account_id, from_account_name, to_account_id, to_account_name, amount: Money, note: string|null, business_date, created_by_name, created_at }`. `CashTransferList = paginated(CashTransfer)`; `CashTransferListQuery` (strict): `account_id?`, `from?`, `to?`, `page`, `pageSize`.
- `CashMovementType` (the baseline enum). `CashLedgerRow`: `{ id, business_date, created_at, type, amount: Money (signed), balance_after: Money, source_type, source_id, source_number: string|null, note: string|null, created_by_name }`.
- `CashLedger`: `{ account: CashAccount, from, to, opening_balance: Money, closing_balance: Money, items: CashLedgerRow[], page, pageSize, total }`; `CashLedgerQuery` (strict): `from?`, `to?` (from ≤ to), `page`, `pageSize`.
- New error codes: `CASH_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` (404), `CASH_ACCOUNT_NAME_TAKEN` (409), `CASH_ACCOUNT_HAS_BALANCE` (409), `CASH_ACCOUNT_IS_DEFAULT` (409), `CASH_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE` (409), `CASH_ACCOUNT_HAS_MOVEMENTS` (409), `TRANSFER_SAME_ACCOUNT` (400). Reuse `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `DAY_CLOSED`, `BRANCH_*`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
