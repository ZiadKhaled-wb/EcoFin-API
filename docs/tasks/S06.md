## Goal
The cashier's main screen. A cashier with an open shift finds products by name, SKU, barcode or a scanned serial, fills a cart, picks a customer, gives discounts, parks a cart and takes it back, and pays, all without touching the mouse. The server still prices everything: the screen only shows what the quote answers.

## Decisions (record them in D66; the ones marked *default* are for the owner to confirm)
1. **Client only.** Every number on the screen comes from the quote or the sale. No money arithmetic in the browser except what `PaymentDialog` already does with the contracts' money helpers.
2. **Scanner = a keyboard scanner** (USB or Bluetooth, types the code and presses Enter). No library is needed, so nothing is bundled and nothing comes from a CDN. *Default:* camera scanning is not built; if the owner wants it later it must be an npm dependency, never a script tag.
3. **Held carts live on the device** (`localStorage`), per shop and employee, at most 10. They reserve no stock and keep no prices: a resumed cart is quoted again. *Default:* no server table, so a held cart is not visible on another till.
4. **The cart on screen survives a refresh** (`sessionStorage`), stored **with its idempotency key**, so a refresh in the middle of a sale and a second Pay is still one sale.
5. **Over-cap discounts are found by the server at the sale** (`DISCOUNT_ABOVE_CAP`); the screen then offers to ask a manager. The quote does not check the cap and the client never computes one.
6. **Not ported from the old POS:** the sale type switch (cash / shipping / installments), browser price and credit maths. Installments are N02.

## Behaviour
1. **Layout.** Inside the normal shell (F09). Two panes that fill the height: products (search box, category chips, result tiles) and the cart (customer, lines, totals, actions). On a narrow screen the panes stack, cart second. RTL and LTR. No open shift → the existing notice and nothing else.
2. **Find products.**
   - The search box has the focus when the page opens and gets it back after every add, dialog close and completed sale.
   - Typing searches by name / SKU / barcode (the existing debounced `productsApi.list`), 24 tiles per page with `keepPreviousData`. With an empty search the first 24 active products of the chosen category (or of all) are shown.
   - Category chips from `categoriesApi.list` (active ones, "All" first); a chip sets `category_id`.
   - A tile shows the name, SKU and `cash_price`. No stock numbers on tiles (the quote reports `available` per cart line).
   - Arrow keys move between tiles, Enter adds the focused one.
3. **Scan.** Enter in the search box with a non-empty text is a scan:
   - `productsApi.lookup(code)` → not serialized: add it, or **raise its quantity by 1 when it is already in the cart** (today `addProduct` ignores a repeat: change that); serialized: open the unit picker for it.
   - `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND` → `unitsApi.find(code)`: a unit that is `in_stock` in the shift's branch and not in the cart is added as its own line; any other unit → a clear message (sold, another branch, already in the cart). Check the fields of `UnitDetail` in the contracts; read the product with `productsApi.get` if you need its name.
   - Nothing found → "no product or serial with this code", the text stays selected so the next scan replaces it.
   - When exactly one tile matches the typed text and the lookup fails, Enter adds that tile.
   - A scan while a request is still running is queued, never dropped; ten quick scans of the same barcode end as quantity 10.
   - Characters typed while no input, button or dialog has the focus go to the search box.
4. **Cart.** The existing lines plus: `−` / `+` buttons beside the quantity, the newest line scrolled into view, the over-stock badge (existing), remove. Serialized lines stay quantity 1 with the serial shown. The price field stays inside `<Can permission="price.override">`.
5. **Customer.**
   - A row at the top of the cart: "Walk-in customer" or the chosen customer's name and main phone, with **Change** and **Clear**. With a customer, show the store credit balance the quote reports when it is above 0.
   - The picker is a dialog: a search box (name, phone, national ID; 300 ms debounce, 2+ characters), up to 10 results with name, phone and `national_id_masked` (never ask for or show the full ID here), a `blocked` badge (a blocked customer can still buy for cash; it is information).
   - **New customer** inside `<Can permission="customer.create">`: name and one phone, `customersApi.create`, then selected. Show its validation errors on the fields.
   - `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN` from the list → hide the customer row for this session (the plan has no customers); sales stay walk-in.
   - `?customer=<id>` (after `Uuid.safeParse`) still selects the customer when the page opens.
6. **Discounts.**
   - Per line: a small control "Discount" → amount or percent and a value (the contracts' `Discount` shape; text in, never a JS number sent as money). The whole sale: the same control under the totals.
   - One **reason** field, shown and required (3 to 200 characters) as soon as any discount is set; **Confirm** is disabled without it.
   - The quote gets `lines[].discount` and `sale_discount`; the sale also gets `discount_reason` (and `discount_approval_id` when there is one). Show per line the discount amount and effective percent, and `discount_total` in the totals, all from the quote.
   - `DISCOUNT_INVALID` from the quote → the message on the cart, Confirm disabled.
   - **Above the cap:** the sale answers 403 `DISCOUNT_ABOVE_CAP` with `{ line_no, cap_pct, effective_pct }`. Close the payment dialog and show a panel: "this discount (x %) is above your limit (y %)" with **Ask a manager** and **Change the discount**.
   - **Ask a manager:** `requestDiscountApproval({ branch_id: shift.branch_id, lines, sale_discount, reason })`. `APPROVAL_NOT_NEEDED` → go on to payment. Otherwise the panel shows "waiting for a manager" and reads `getDiscountApproval(id)` every 3 seconds (stop when the page unmounts or the request is over).
     - `approved` → "approved by {name}"; Confirm now sends the sale with `discount_approval_id`.
     - `rejected` → the manager's note; the cashier changes the discount.
     - **Cancel request** → `cancelDiscountApproval(id)`.
   - Any change to the lines, the discounts or the customer drops the approval (cancel it first when it is still pending) and the cashier asks again.
   - `APPROVAL_INVALID` at the sale (15 minutes passed, or the cart is not the approved one) → drop the approval and say so.
   - A user with `discount.above_cap` never sees the panel: the server lets the sale through.
7. **Hold and resume.**
   - **Hold** (cart not empty): stores the lines (with typed quantities, prices and discounts), the customer (id and name), the sale discount, the reason, the time and an optional short note, then empties the cart. Not stored: the quote, the approval, the idempotency key.
   - **Held carts** opens a list (time, customer or walk-in, number of items, note) with **Resume** and **Delete**. Resume is offered only while the current cart is empty, takes the cart out of the list, gives it a new key and quotes it. The button shows the count.
   - At 10 held carts Hold is refused with a message.
   - Storage keys carry the org id and the employee id; another user on the same browser sees none of them. Read with a zod schema; anything that does not parse is dropped silently. Every storage call is in try/catch, and the screen works without storage.
   - The active cart is written to `sessionStorage` on every change (Decision 4) and removed by a completed sale, **New sale** and Hold.
8. **Pay.** **Confirm and pay** opens `PaymentDialog` (unchanged rules). Add only: the focus starts in the cash line's **Received** field, and Enter pays when **Pay** is enabled. Empty Received = exact cash, as today.
9. **After the sale.** The existing done card (number, total, payments, change). Enter or **New sale** starts the next cart with the focus in the search box. Leave one empty, commented slot for S04's print button; don't build printing.
10. **Shortcuts** (one hook, one `keydown` listener on `window`, `preventDefault` on the keys it handles; while a dialog is open only Esc and that dialog's own keys work):

    | Key | Action |
    |---|---|
    | F2 | focus the search box |
    | F4 | customer picker |
    | F7 | sale discount |
    | F8 | hold the cart |
    | F9 | held carts |
    | F10 | confirm and pay |
    | Esc | close the open dialog or panel |
    | Enter | scan / add (search box), pay (payment dialog), new sale (done card) |

    Each button shows its key as a hint, and a small "Shortcuts" help lists them. Don't use F1, F3, F5, F6, F11 or F12 (the browser keeps them).
11. **Speed on cheap hardware.**
    - No new dependency. No request between **Pay** and the done card except the sale itself (cache invalidation stays after `setDone`, as now).
    - The product pane must not re-render when only the cart changes, and the cart must not re-render on every search keystroke (split the components, memoise the tile list).
    - Keep the 250 ms quote debounce; never fire one quote per scan in a burst.
12. **Errors.** Everything through `saleError`; add keys only for new messages (scan not found, unit not available here, held carts full, approval states). Arabic and English, same keys.
