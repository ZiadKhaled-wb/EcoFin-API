## Goal
A shop sets a purchase approval threshold in Settings (it already exists, see below). When a manager approves an invoice whose total is above the threshold, it doesn't post. It goes to **"waiting for owner approval"**, and only the **owner** approves or rejects it, with a reason. A list shows the waiting invoices.

## Slice text (U06)
- **Includes:** thresholds in settings (e.g. above X needs the owner); invoices above the threshold wait in an approval list; approvals and rejections are audited with a reason.
- **Done when:** a user below the threshold can't approve a large invoice; the approval list shows waiting invoices.

## Decisions already made (record them in D55)
1. **User decision:** above the threshold, **only the OWNER** approves or rejects. Below it (or with no threshold), anyone with `purchase.invoice_approve` approves, as in U02. **No new permission key.**
2. **The threshold** is `org_settings.purchase_approval_threshold`:
   - It is already editable in `GET/PATCH /api/settings` and on the Web settings page (P03). **Don't touch settings.**
   - `NULL` = no approval step.
   - The comparison is **strictly greater**: `total > threshold`, on the invoice **total including VAT**, after U02 recomputes the lines at approval.
3. **What happens when a non-owner approves an invoice above the threshold:**
   - the invoice goes `draft` → `pending_approval`;
   - one `approvals` row is created (kind `purchase_invoice`, status `pending`);
   - the audit row is `purchase_invoice.approval_requested`;
   - the call **succeeds**, returns the invoice with status `pending_approval`, and posts **no stock and no cost**.
4. **A pending invoice:**
   - it can't be edited (U02's draft-only rule; `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_EDITABLE`);
   - a non-owner calling approve again → `APPROVAL_OWNER_ONLY` (403);
   - the owner approves it with a **note** (required, 3–500 characters, else `APPROVAL_NOTE_REQUIRED`); this posts exactly as U02 does and marks the approval `approved`;
   - the owner rejects it with a **reason** (U02's reject; required); this marks the invoice `rejected` and the approval `rejected`.
5. **The owner approving a draft above the threshold** approves it directly: no pending step, no note needed. The audit row records `{threshold, above: true}`.
6. **A pending invoice stays pending** even if the threshold is later removed or raised. Only the owner's decision ends it.
7. **The approval list** is visible to holders of `purchase.invoice_approve` (OWNER included), for the branches they work in (OWNER: all). Only the OWNER sees the approve and reject buttons, and the server enforces it.

## Part A: server + contracts
1. **Migration** `20261011110000_u06_purchase_approval.sql`:
   - A partial unique index: one pending approval per invoice, `approvals (organization_id, entity_id) WHERE kind = 'purchase_invoice' AND status = 'pending'`.
   - `DROP FUNCTION public.approve_purchase_invoice(uuid)`, then create `public.approve_purchase_invoice(p_id uuid, p_note text DEFAULT NULL)`. It keeps **all** of U02's behaviour (idempotent when already approved, serial checks, the recompute, posting order) and adds decisions 3–6 at U02's `-- U06: threshold check goes here` comment:
     - **Draft:**
       - if the threshold is set, `total > threshold` and the caller isn't OWNER: set `pending_approval`; insert the approval (`branch_id`, `amount = total`, `payload {threshold, number}`, `requested_by`); audit `purchase_invoice.approval_requested`; return.
       - otherwise: post as U02. The audit includes `{threshold, above}`.
     - **Pending:**
       - caller not OWNER → `APPROVAL_OWNER_ONLY`;
       - `p_note` missing or shorter than 3 → `APPROVAL_NOTE_REQUIRED`;
       - else post as U02, set the approval `approved` (`decided_by`, `decided_at`, `decision_note = note`), and audit `purchase_invoice.approved` with the note as `reason`.
   - `public.reject_purchase_invoice`: when the invoice is pending, the caller must be OWNER (`APPROVAL_OWNER_ONLY`), and the approval row becomes `rejected` with `decision_note = reason`, in the same transaction.
   - `public.list_purchase_approvals(p_status text, p_branch_id uuid, p_limit int, p_offset int)`:
     - `has_permission('purchase.invoice_approve')` (a read: works in an expired shop);
     - the caller's branches only;
     - newest first.
