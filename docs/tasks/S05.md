## Goal
A customer brings goods back. The cashier opens the original sale, picks the lines and quantities (the exact serial for a serialized item), says why and in what condition, and the shop gives the right money or credit back: in the method the customer paid with, or as store credit; for an installment sale, by lowering what is still owed on the contract and spreading it over the remaining installments, and paying back only what was overpaid. An exchange is a return and a new sale posted together, settling the difference. Stock, cash and tax each change **once**; the ETA gets a return receipt linked to the sale's receipt. Nothing is edited or deleted: a return is its own document.

## Decisions (record them in D88)
1. **One document per return, one transaction.** `POST /api/returns` → `public.post_sales_return(p_body jsonb, p_idempotency_key text)`. It writes one `sales_returns` row (number `RT` from `app.next_document_number(org, branch, 'sales_return')`), its `sales_return_lines`, the stock rows, the refund rows, the contract change (installment sale), the fiscal row and the audit, or nothing. Follow `void_sale`'s shape: idempotency lock and replay first (same key, other body → `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`; a `system:` key → `VALIDATION_FAILED`), then locks in one fixed order (sale → its lines by product, id → the sale's stock movements → stock levels by product, state → units by id → the contract and its current lines → the fiscal row), then the checks, then the writes. Every refusal leaves nothing and takes no number.
2. **Where a return lives.** In **the caller's open shift** (`SHIFT_REQUIRED`; its device, `app.require_shift_device`), like a sale: the shift gives the branch, the drawer and the device. The sale must be of the **same branch** (`RETURN_OTHER_BRANCH`, 409; owner question 4). `refund.create` + the `pos` module (`app.require_permission`, so an expired shop is refused); a contract sale also needs the `installments` module. The sale must be the shop's and in the caller's branches (`SALE_NOT_FOUND`).
3. **What can come back.** Per sale line: returnable = sold − already returned (all earlier returns of every kind; a voided sale has nothing left → `ALREADY_VOIDED`). More → `RETURN_QTY_EXCEEDED` (409, exists; details `sale_line_id`, `returnable`). A serialized line comes back unit by unit: the body names the unit; it must be **that line's** unit and not returned yet (`RETURN_UNIT_MISMATCH`, 409: "a different serial is rejected"). One body line per unit; quantity 1. A line can't appear twice for the same unit. 1-200 lines.
4. **Return period.** `sale.business_date + settings.return_policy_days >= today` (shop time zone, `app.org_today`). Later → `RETURN_PERIOD_EXPIRED` (409, details `deadline`), unless the caller is the **OWNER** (owner question 2) or the return names a warranty claim of that line (decision 11). Reason required (3-200, `REASON_REQUIRED`).
5. **Amounts come from the stored sale, never from the client.** Per returned unit of a line: `refund = line_total / quantity`, `tax = tax_amount / quantity`, `cost = unit_cost` (non-serialized: the line's `unit_cost`; serialized: the unit's line cost), rounded to the piaster; **the last unit of a line takes the rest**, so a line returned in pieces refunds exactly `line_total` and `tax_amount` in total (S03 discounts are already inside `line_total`). `sales_returns.subtotal / tax_total / total` = the sums. The body carries `expected_total` (the quote the cashier confirmed); a different server total → `PRICE_CHANGED` (409, both totals), as S01.
6. **Stock goes back once.** One `stock_movements` row per returned line (per unit for serials): type `sale_return_in`, source `sales_return` / the return line, `reverses_movement_id` = the sale's `sale_out` row, `unit_cost` = decision 5's cost, in the sale's branch, `stock_state` = `sellable` for condition `resellable`, `damaged` for `damaged`. A serialized unit: check what the I04 trigger does on `sale_return_in` and make it end `in_stock` (resellable) or `returned` in damaged stock (not sellable); test both. Damaged stock is never sold (S01 sells sellable only) and is not adjusted by I0x (D46 note). Moving average: follow what the ledger trigger already does for `sale_return_in` at the original cost; don't add a second rule.
7. **Money back on a cash sale (no contract).** The body's `refunds` lines (1-5) must add up to `total` exactly (`PAYMENT_TOTAL_MISMATCH`, 400, as S02). Allowed methods (owner question 1):
   - **The sale's own methods**, each up to what the sale was paid by that method minus what earlier returns already refunded by it (`REFUND_EXCEEDS_PAID`, 409, details `method`, `available`): `cash` out of the **caller's drawer** (movement type `refund`, tagged with the shift; check whether K02 refuses a drawer below zero and map it to a clear error, say which in D88); `bank_transfer` back out of **the bank the sale was paid into** with a `reference` (1-100); `store_credit` back to the customer's credit.
   - **`store_credit` for any part**, whatever the sale was paid with, when the sale has a customer (`VALIDATION_FAILED` without one).
   Each line = one `payments` row (`direction out`, `PY` number, key `system:return:<return id>:<n>`, the caller's shift) + one `payment_allocations` row with `return_id` + its ledger row (`cash_movements` `-amount` type `refund` source `sales_return`; or `store_credit_movements` `+amount` source `sales_return`). `sales_returns.refund_method` = the line with the largest amount (as `void_sale` does); the payments carry the detail.
8. **Installment sale (the slice's Done when).** A sale with a contract (`contracts.sale_id`) returns through the contract, by rule 1 of `ISLAMIC_FINANCE_RULES.md` (one price fixed at signing; a partial return removes the returned goods' share of that price, nothing is added):
   - `share` = this return's `total` / the sale's `total`. The customer's **credit** = `total` + round(`share` × `contract.markup_amount`) (the returned goods' cash price plus their part of the profit).
   - The credit first lowers **what is still owed** on the current version (`app.contract_left`: remaining principal and profit). New remaining = max(remaining − credit, 0). New remaining profit = max(remaining profit − round(`share` × `markup_amount`), 0), capped at the new remaining; principal = the rest (rule 9: the split is accounting only).
   - New remaining > 0: write version `current_version + 1` over **the same number of remaining (not fully paid) installments and their due dates**, each lowered (`app.schedule_split`'s arithmetic: rounded to the policy step, the last takes the rest, profit in proportion; owner question 3), through `app.write_schedule_version`, with one `contract_changes` row: kind `return_adjustment`, `sales_return_id`, `amount` = the part of the credit applied to the contract, `principal_amount` / `profit_amount` = the new version's sums, `profit_waived` 0, the return's reason and key. `app.schedule_lines_sum_check` must accept it unchanged (it checks version > 1 against that row). Status stays (`active`, or `rescheduled` when it was).
   - New remaining = 0: the lines left are superseded with no new version only if N05's mechanism allows it; otherwise a version of zero lines is not possible, so close it the way N05's settlement does (read `app.write_schedule_version`) and set status **`returned`** when every unit of the sale has now come back, **`completed`** otherwise. The excess (credit − what was owed) is **overpaid**: it goes back to the customer through `refunds` lines (cash from the drawer, bank transfer, or store credit; the customer is always known on a contract sale). `refunds` must add up to the overpaid amount exactly (0 lines when nothing is overpaid).
   - `sales_returns.refund_method` = `contract_adjustment` when nothing was paid back, else the largest refund line's method.
   - A `written_off` contract → `CONTRACT_NOT_OPEN` (409; reuse a code if N05 has one). A return after a collection blocks that collection's reversal (N05 already refuses a reversal on a contract changed after it).
   - **Worked example (put it in the SQL test and D88):** items A 6 000.00 and B 4 000.00, sale total 10 000.00, down payment 1 000.00, financed 9 000.00, profit 1 800.00, 12 monthly lines of 900.00 (750.00 + 150.00). Two collections paid (1 800.00). Return B: share 0.4, credit 4 000.00 + 720.00 = 4 720.00; remaining 9 000.00 → **4 280.00** (principal 3 500.00, profit 780.00) over the 10 remaining lines = **428.00** each. Check: A alone at the installment price = 6 000.00 + 1 080.00 = 7 080.00; paid 2 800.00; left 4 280.00. Stock +1 B, cash 0, tax −(B's VAT) once.
9. **The customer account stays right (R02, D74 decision 5).** After this slice, `app.customer_open_balance` must equal Σ `v_contract_balances.remaining` on every customer (R02's SQL test checks it): the new version already carries the lower amount, so the open balance must **stop** subtracting `contract_adjustment` returns a second time (fix the formula, close D74's `TODO(S05/N05)`). The statement shows the return as **one credit row** (the credit applied to the contract plus any refund), and the contract's debit row adds the `return_adjustment` amounts back so history does not move (N05's waiver pattern). R02's tests must pass unchanged except the cases this fixes; say which in D88.
10. **Tax and the ETA.** The return's `tax_total` is the VAT reversed (decision 5). When the sale has a fiscal row (`fiscal_submissions.sale_id`) and the shop has the `eta` module, insert one `fiscal_submissions` row: `doc_type b2c_return`, `return_id`, the **return's shift device**, `internal_id` = the return's number, status `pending`, and queue `eta.prepare` the way T02's trigger does for a sale. Wire `src/eta/build-return.ts` into `prepare.ts` (`referenceUUID` = the sale receipt's `eta_uuid`; the return is its own link in that device's chain). If the sale's receipt has **no UUID yet**, the return is still posted; its fiscal row waits (prepare skips it until the sale's receipt has a UUID; the T03 monitor shows it as pending). Sale receipt issued more than 540 days ago → `RETURN_ETA_WINDOW_PASSED` (409) before anything is written. A `cancelled` sale receipt (a void) can't happen here (decision 3). The receipt amounts come from the stored return lines (ETA amounts exclude VAT, as T02).
11. **Warranty refunds (S12).** The body may name `warranty_claim_id`: the claim must be open on one of the returned lines' units (`VALIDATION_FAILED` otherwise); then decision 4's period does not apply, and the claim moves to `closed` with "refunded by RT-…" in its resolution and an audit row. `request_claim_refund` stops answering `CLAIM_REFUND_NEEDS_RETURN` blindly: it answers `{ sale_id, sale_line_id, unit_id }` so the Web opens the return form filled (keep its key `warranty.manage`); update S12's tests.
12. **Exchange = a return + a new sale, one transaction.** `kind = 'exchange'` with a `new_sale` object (the S01/S02 sale body: lines, discounts, customer = the original's, `expected_total`, `payments`) → the return (decisions 3-7) and `app.post_sale_core` for the new sale in the same shift, `sales_returns.exchange_sale_id` = the new sale. The return's credit pays the new sale first: one `refund` cash movement out of the drawer and one `sale` cash movement in, both for the credit used (net 0 in the drawer, each document balanced: K04 shows one return and one sale). Then the difference: new sale > credit → the customer pays it by the new sale's `payments` lines; credit > new sale → the rest goes back by `refunds` lines (decision 7). No contract sale on either side (`EXCHANGE_NOT_FOR_CONTRACT`, 409; owner question 5). If the ETA path needs both documents, both get their fiscal rows (return receipt + sale receipt). If a cleaner settlement shows up while building (no paired drawer rows), use it and say why in D88.
13. **Reads.** Who can read a return: whoever can read its sale (S01/S07 rule: `sale.create` own, `report.view` / `cash.view` the caller's branches), plus `refund.create` in the caller's branches. Reads work in an expired shop; not audited.

## Owner questions (confirmed 22 Oct: the recommended answer of each, record them in D88)
1. **Refund method on a cash sale:** the sale's own methods up to what each paid, or store credit for any part, *or* store credit only, *or* always cash. → **Own methods or store credit.**
2. **After the return period** (`return_policy_days`, default 14): the owner only (with the reason, a warning audit), *or* a manager's approval on the spot (S03's approval flow with kind `refund`), *or* never. → **Owner only.**
3. **Installment return, what changes:** the same remaining installments and dates, each smaller, *or* the same installment amount and fewer installments (the last ones dropped). → **Same dates, smaller installments.**
4. **Return at another branch:** refused (go to the selling branch), *or* allowed (stock comes back to the returning branch, cash from its drawer). → **Refused in S05.**
5. **Exchange on an installment sale:** not allowed (return, then a new installment sale), *or* allowed. → **Not allowed.**
6. **Damaged returns:** kept as damaged stock in the branch (not sellable; written off or sent to the supplier later), *or* written off at once. → **Kept as damaged stock.**
