## Goal
Every installment sale gets its terms from one calculation on the server: from the shop's current policy (P03) and the cashier's inputs (cash price, down payment, number of installments, frequency, first due date) it produces the financed amount, the markup, the total payable and the schedule lines (each split into principal and profit), all adding up exactly. The POS asks for a **preview** before confirming (with the customer's credit, guarantor and survey requirements); N02's confirm step **recalculates** on the server and stores what this engine says. A contract whose terms break the policy, or whose lines don't add up, is refused at commit even when a client writes the tables directly.

## Slice text (N01)
- **Includes:** reads the shop's policy (P03): min down payment %, max months, markup % (flat or by duration tiers), allowed frequencies, minimum installment amount, rounding; input price, down payment, number of installments, frequency, start date → output financed amount, markup, total payable, schedule lines (principal + profit), all adding up exactly; validates against the customer's limit (R03), status, guarantor and survey requirements (R04, R05); a preview endpoint for the POS; the confirm step recalculates on the server.
- **Done when:** schedule lines always sum to the total payable; changing the policy changes new contracts only; the server rejects terms that break the policy.

## Decisions (record them in D76; the ones marked *to confirm* are my defaults: post them on EC-80 for the owner **before you start**, and build on the defaults unless the owner answers otherwise)
1. **One calculation, in SQL, with a TypeScript copy (D41's pattern).** `app.installment_terms(p_policy public.installment_policies, p_input jsonb, p_today date) RETURNS jsonb` is the source of truth: pure (no table reads, no raise for a broken rule), `IMMUTABLE`-safe inputs only. `computeInstallmentTerms(policy, input, today)` in the contracts is the same calculation for screens (instant feedback while the cashier types). Both run the same golden cases (`supabase/tests/fixtures/n01_terms_cases.json`, at least 30 cases); the SQL test embeds a copy and `src/test/n01-golden.test.ts` fails if the copies differ (copy `t01-golden.test.ts`). Money is `numeric` in SQL and decimal strings / big.js in TS, never floats.
2. **Inputs:** `cash_price` (Money > 0: the price the installment sale is based on; which product price feeds it is N02's), `down_payment` (Money ≥ 0), `installments_count` (int 1-520, the `contracts` CHECK), `frequency` (`monthly | weekly | daily`), `first_due_date` (optional ISO date; default below). `p_today` = `app.org_today(org)` (shop timezone) on the server, passed in by the caller of the pure function.
3. **Amounts:** `financed_amount = cash_price − down_payment`. `markup_pct` = the `pct` of the **first** `markup_tiers` entry whose `up_to_months ≥ duration_months` (a flat markup is one tier). `markup_amount = round_half_up(financed × markup_pct / 100, 2)`. `total_payable = financed + markup`. **`pct` is the markup for the whole term, not per month** (*to confirm*: the alternative is "% per month × months").
4. **Duration in months** (for the markup tier, `max_months` and `amount_tiers`): monthly = `installments_count`; weekly = `ceil(installments_count × 7 / 30)`; daily = `ceil(installments_count / 30)` (*to confirm*).
5. **Installment amount and rounding:** `installment_amount` = `total_payable / installments_count` rounded **up** to a multiple of `rounding_step` (smallest multiple ≥ the exact share; with one installment it is the total). Lines 1..n−1 carry it; the **last line** carries `total_payable − (n−1) × installment_amount` (so it is smaller or equal). A last line ≤ 0 is the violation `rounding` (too many installments for this step). The total and the markup never move because of rounding (*to confirm*: the alternative is "all lines equal, the markup absorbs the rounding").
6. **Principal and profit per line:** the markup is spread over the lines in proportion to their amounts, largest remainder in piasters (leftovers to the larger remainders, ties to the earlier line), `profit_i` = that share, `principal_i = amount_i − profit_i`. So Σ amount = total payable, Σ principal = financed, Σ profit = markup, every value ≥ 0 (*to confirm*: proportional ("flat") profit; the alternative "profit first" changes how much an early settlement (N05) saves).
7. **Due dates:** `first_due_date` defaults to one period after today (monthly: same day next month, month-end clamped; weekly: +7 days; daily: +1 day). Allowed: after today and at most **60 days** after today (*to confirm*). Line k (1-based) is due on `first_due_date + (k−1) periods`, monthly lines **computed from the first date** each time (`first + make_interval(months => k−1)`, so 31 Jan → 28 Feb (29 in a leap year) → 31 Mar → 30 Apr), weekly +7 days, daily +1 day. The TS copy must match Postgres' month-end clamping (golden cases cover it).
8. **Policy rules (each failure is one entry in `violations`, the calculation still returns the numbers when it can):**
   - `frequency_not_allowed`: frequency not in `allowed_frequencies`.
   - `sale_below_minimum`: `cash_price < min_sale_amount`.
   - `down_payment_too_high`: `down_payment ≥ cash_price`, or above `round_half_up(cash_price × max_down_payment_pct / 100, 2)` when that is set.
   - `down_payment_too_low`: below the minimum of the frequency's `down_payment_rules` entry (a frequency without an entry uses `percent`):
     - `percent`: `round_half_up(cash_price × min_down_payment_pct / 100, 2)`;
     - `one_installment`: one `installment_amount` (as computed with this down payment);
     - `installment_per_day_of_month` (daily only): `installment_amount × day of month of today` (sale on the 10th → 10 installments).
     The installment rules **replace** `min_down_payment_pct` for that frequency, they don't add to it (*to confirm*).
   - `too_many_months`: `duration_months` > `max_months`, or > the `max_months` of the first `amount_tiers` entry whose `up_to_amount ≥ cash_price` (above the last tier: `max_months`). Details carry `max_months` allowed.
   - `installment_below_minimum`: `installment_amount` < max(`min_installment`, `min_installment_by_frequency[frequency]` or 0). The last (remainder) line is exempt (*to confirm*).
   - `rounding`: decision 5.
   - `first_due_date_out_of_range`: decision 7.
   Body shape errors (count outside 1-520, negative money, unknown frequency) stay `VALIDATION_FAILED` from the contract schema, before the engine.
9. **Answer of the engine** (`InstallmentTerms`, both copies): `{ policy_id, policy_version, cash_price, down_payment, financed_amount, markup_pct, markup_amount, total_payable, installments_count, frequency, duration_months, installment_amount, last_installment_amount, first_due_date, last_due_date, min_down_payment, max_down_payment, max_months_allowed, lines: [{ seq, due_date, principal, profit, amount }], violations: [{ rule, ...details }] }`. `min_down_payment` = the smallest down payment that passes `down_payment_too_low` for these inputs (for the installment rules: the smallest amount in piasters, found by a bounded search; null when none passes below the cash price), so the POS can show "at least X". `lines` is empty when a violation makes them meaningless (`rounding`, `down_payment_too_high`).
10. **The confirm side for N02 (no route of its own; not granted to `authenticated`):** `app.check_installment_terms(p_org uuid, p_input jsonb, p_policy_version integer) RETURNS jsonb`: loads `app.current_installment_policy(p_org)` (none → `POLICY_NOT_SET`, 409); `p_policy_version` (what the preview showed) differs from the current version → `POLICY_CHANGED` (409, `{ current_version }`) so the POS re-previews instead of silently selling on new terms; runs `app.installment_terms` with `app.org_today`; any violation → `INSTALLMENT_TERMS_INVALID` (400, `{ violations }`); returns the terms. N02 inserts the contract and its version-1 `schedule_lines` from exactly this answer, in its transaction.
11. **Safety net (any writer):** `CONSTRAINT TRIGGER trg_contracts_terms_check AFTER INSERT ON public.contracts DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED FOR EACH ROW`, skipped for `is_migrated` contracts. At commit:
    - the contract's `policy_id` must be the shop's current version at that moment (`POLICY_CHANGED`): "changing the policy changes new contracts only";
    - recompute `app.installment_terms` from the contract's own columns and policy, with today = the shop date of `created_at`: any violation → `INSTALLMENT_TERMS_INVALID`; `markup_pct`, `markup_amount`, `total_payable`, `installments_count`, `first_due_date` must equal the recomputed ones, and the contract's version-1 lines must equal the recomputed lines (seq, due date, principal, profit) → otherwise `INSTALLMENT_TERMS_INVALID` with `{ mismatch: '<field>' }`.
    And `CONSTRAINT TRIGGER trg_schedule_lines_sum_check AFTER INSERT ON public.schedule_lines DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED FOR EACH ROW` (all contracts, migrated ones too): at commit, for the inserted line's contract and version, Σ amount = the contract total of that version (version 1: `total_payable`; a later version is N05's: compare with what N05 stores, so leave a `TODO(N05)` and check version 1 only now), Σ principal = `financed_amount`, Σ profit = `markup_amount`, seq is 1..n without gaps → else `SCHEDULE_NOT_BALANCED` (500-class internal error code, 409 in the contract). A policy published after a contract never touches it: contracts keep their `policy_id` and lines (tested).
12. **Preview** `public.preview_installment_terms(p_body jsonb) RETURNS jsonb` (granted to `authenticated`) behind `POST /api/installments/preview`:
    - `app.require_permission('installment.create')`, `app.require_module('installments')`, the shop's `installments_enabled` (`INSTALLMENTS_DISABLED`), a current policy (`POLICY_NOT_SET`). Read only: an expired (read-only) shop may preview (*to confirm*); N02's write refuses it.
    - Body: the inputs of decision 2 plus optional `customer_id` (the POS can preview before picking the customer) and optional `guarantor_ids` (what the cashier picked so far).
    - Answer `InstallmentPreview`: `{ terms: InstallmentTerms, customer: null | { credit, guarantors, survey }, can_confirm: boolean }` where, with a customer, `credit` is the R03 preview answer (`get_customer_credit_check` for the financed amount), `guarantors` the R04 requirement answer (`get_guarantor_requirement` for the financed amount, plus `given` = the count of `guarantor_ids` that pass `app.check_contract_guarantors`' rules, and `met`), `survey` the R05 requirement answer (`get_survey_requirement`). Call those functions, don't copy their logic; their guards apply (`CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`, `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED` raise as there). `can_confirm` = no violations and, with a customer, credit allowed (or an approved exception exists), guarantors met and survey met; without a customer it is false.
    - No audit row (nothing is written).
13. **No table, column, enum or permission change.** New error codes only: `INSTALLMENT_TERMS_INVALID` (400), `POLICY_NOT_SET` (409), `POLICY_CHANGED` (409), `SCHEDULE_NOT_BALANCED` (409). Reuse `INSTALLMENTS_DISABLED`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN`, `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`, `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`.
14. **VAT on the markup** (`org_settings.installment_markup_vat`, T01) is **not** applied here: the engine works on amounts as given. N02 decides with T01 how the markup is taxed (open question for the accountant, D41). Say so in D76.
