## Goal
At the end of each business day, a manager opens **Day close** for their branch and sees, from the server: the day's sales by payment method, returns and voids, installment collections, expenses, supplier payments, every cash account's opening and closing balance with what moved, every shift with its expected / counted / difference, and the ETA status of the day's receipts. Under the numbers, a list of **issues**, each with a link to its cause (an open shift, a shift difference, a receipt ETA has not accepted, a cached balance that does not match its ledger, a document without its ledger row). The manager closes the day; the numbers are stored as a snapshot, and from then on the database refuses any new entry dated that day for that branch. The owner can reopen a day with a reason. Every night a job runs the same checks on yesterday and on any closed day, and the dashboard shows what it found.

## How the baseline already works (don't rebuild it)
- `day_closes` (one row per shop, branch, date; `status closed | reopened`; `totals`, `issues` jsonb; `closed_by/at`; `reopened_by/at`, `reopen_reason`; CHECK: `status = 'reopened'` exactly when `reopened_at` and `reopen_reason` are set).
- `app.check_business_date()` runs BEFORE INSERT on `cash_movements`, `payments`, `sales`, `sales_returns`, `expenses`, `stock_movements`, `supplier_payments`, `purchase_returns`, `cash_transfers`, `store_credit_movements`: it takes the day lock **shared** and refuses a row dated in a `closed` day (`DAY_CLOSED`, already mapped to 409). `app.lock_day_for_close()` takes the same lock **exclusive**. So K04 only has to insert/update the `day_closes` row while holding the exclusive lock; nothing can slip into the day while it is being closed.
- No function accepts a business date from the caller today: every document is dated "today in the shop's timezone". "Back-dated entries" therefore only happen in a reopened day (and later in M02 / V2 corrections). The rule "a closed day can't take back-dated entries without permission" is: refused always (`DAY_CLOSED`); the holder of `day.reopen` (owner only by default) reopens the day with a reason, the entry is made, the day is closed again (decision 6). No new permission key.
- Permissions: `day.close` (OWNER, BRANCH_MANAGER, ACCOUNTANT by role default), `day.reopen` (OWNER only: excluded from BRANCH_MANAGER, not granted to ACCOUNTANT). Module `cash`.

## Decisions (record them in D84)
1. **One computation, three users.** `app.day_close_compute(p_org, p_branch_id, p_date) RETURNS jsonb` builds `{ totals, issues }` for a branch and day from the ledgers and documents. The preview (route 2), the close (route 3) and the nightly job (decision 9) all call it, so what the manager saw, what is stored and what the job checks are the same numbers. Money as strings (numeric(18,2)), never floats.
2. **Totals** (all for `branch_id = p_branch_id AND business_date = p_date`):
   - `sales`: `count`, `total`, `discount_total`, `tax_total`, `paid_by_method { cash, bank_transfer, store_credit }` (the day's `payments` `direction = 'in'` allocated to these sales, by method), `on_contract` (the part of installment sales' totals financed by an N02 contract = total − paid now), `voided { count, total }` (sales with a `sales_returns` row of `kind = 'void'` dated this day). A voided sale stays counted in `count`/`total` on its own day; the void is shown on the void's day (documents are never edited).
   - `returns` (S05; zeros until it is built): `count`, `total`, `refunded_by_method` (payments `direction = 'out'` with a `return_id` allocation).
   - `collections` (N03 / N06): `count`, `total`, `principal`, `profit`, `by_method`, `in_field` (collected into a collector wallet), `reversed { count, total }` (N05 reversals dated this day).
   - `expenses` (K03): `count`, `total`, `by_category [{ category_id, name, total }]`, `reversed { count, total }`.
   - `supplier_payments` (U03) and `supplier_refunds` (U04): `count`, `total`.
   - `cash`: one entry per cash account **of the branch** (safes, banks of the branch, every drawer and collector wallet with that branch): `account { id, name, kind }`, `opening` (ledger sum before the day), `in`, `out`, `closing` (ledger sum up to and including the day), `by_type { sale: …, collection: …, expense: …, … }` (`cash_movement_type` keys, only non-zero ones). **Shop-wide banks** (`branch_id IS NULL`) appear as `{ account, in, out, by_type }` with the movements this branch booked on them that day and **no** opening / closing (their balance belongs to the shop, not the branch). `cash.total_closing_cash` = the closing of the branch's safes + drawers + wallets (what physically sits in the branch).
   - `shifts`: every shift of the branch opened on the day or with movements dated the day: `id`, cashier, device, `opened_at`, `closed_at`, `status`, `opening_float`, `expected`, `counted`, `difference`, `difference_reason`, `payment_totals` (S02's shape). `shifts.difference_total`.
   - `eta`: T03's `fiscal_day_summary` for the branch and day (`total`, `accepted`, `waiting`, `rejected`, `send_failed`, `blocked`, `all_accepted`); `null` when the shop has no ETA setup.
3. **Issues:** a list of `{ code, severity, message_key, params, link: { kind, id } | null }`, `severity` one of `blocking`, `warning`, `info`. The Web turns `message_key` + `params` into Arabic text and `link` into a route (shift, sale, payment, expense, transfer, cash account, product, the ETA monitor filtered to the branch and day).
   - `SHIFT_OPEN` (**blocking**): a shift of the branch is still open and was opened on or before the day. The cashier closes it first (K02), or a manager uses the existing `force_close_shift` (EC-147) when the cashier is gone; the issue links to the shift. *Default.*
   - `LEDGER_MISMATCH` (**warning**): `app.ledger_mismatches(org)` rows for a cash account of the branch, a shop-wide bank this branch moved that day, or stock in the branch: cached balance ≠ ledger sum. Link to the account or product. This is "a broken balance is flagged". It does not block the close (the ledger is the truth and the day's numbers come from it), but the closer must acknowledge it (decision 5) and the job raises it every night until fixed.
   - `DOCUMENT_WITHOUT_LEDGER` (**warning**): the day's documents and the day's cash movements disagree. Each family is matched by `source_type` / `source_id`: a cash / bank `payment` without its `cash_movements` row (or with a different amount), an `expense` without its `expense` movement, a `cash_transfer` without both sides, a `supplier_payment` without its movement, and the reverse (a movement whose source document is missing or dated another day). One issue per document (at most 50, then a `params.more` count), link to the document. This is the check behind "the day-close numbers equal the sum of the day's documents".
   - `ETA_NOT_ACCEPTED` (**warning**): `eta.all_accepted = false`; `params` = the counts; link to the ETA monitor (T03) for the branch and day. *Default: a warning, not blocking* (ETA can be down for a day; the receipts still have to go, T03 keeps retrying and alerting).
   - `SHIFT_DIFFERENCE` (**info**): one per shift closed with a difference (cashier, amount, reason), link to the shift.
   - `WALLET_SHORTAGE` (**info**): one per N06 settlement of the day with a shortage, link to the settlement.
   - `EARLIER_DAY_OPEN` (**info**): an earlier day of the branch (last 60 days) has activity and is not closed. *Default: days do not have to be closed in order.*
4. **Which days:** any `p_date ≤` today in the shop's timezone (`FUTURE_BUSINESS_DATE` otherwise, the baseline's code, 422). Closing **today** is allowed; anything the branch then tries to post today gets `DAY_CLOSED` until the owner reopens (the close dialog says so when the date is today). A day with no activity can be closed (all zeros) but is never required.
5. **Closing** (`day.close`, branch access, module `cash`, shop must be able to write): `close_day(p_branch_id, p_date, p_preview_hash, p_note)`. In one transaction: `app.lock_day_for_close` (exclusive), refuse if a `closed` row exists (`DAY_ALREADY_CLOSED`, 409), compute (decision 1), refuse any `blocking` issue (`DAY_CLOSE_BLOCKED`, 409, `{ issues }`), compare the hash of the computed `{ totals, issues }` with `p_preview_hash` (`DAY_CHANGED`, 409, `{ preview }`: something was posted after the manager looked; the screen reloads the preview), require a note (3-500) when any `warning` issue exists (`NOTE_REQUIRED`, 422), then insert the row (or update the `reopened` row back to `closed`, clearing `reopened_at` / `reopened_by` / `reopen_reason` because of the CHECK; the history stays in the audit log) with `totals`, `issues`, `closed_by`, `closed_at`, the note in `totals.note`. Audit `day.closed` (severity `warning` when issues of severity warning were acknowledged). The hash is `md5` of the canonical jsonb text, computed by the server in the preview answer; the client only sends it back. No idempotency key: closing twice is `DAY_ALREADY_CLOSED`, and the screen treats that answer as success after a retry.
6. **Reopening** (`day.reopen`, branch access): `reopen_day(p_id, p_reason)`, reason 3-500, only a `closed` row (`DAY_NOT_CLOSED`, 409). Sets `status = 'reopened'`, `reopened_by/at`, `reopen_reason`; keeps the old `totals` / `issues` on the row until the next close (the screen shows them as "last close" next to the live preview). Audit `day.reopened`, severity `warning`, with the reason and the old totals' hash. The day is then open like any other; closing it again recomputes everything (decision 5).
7. **Reads:** `day.close` **or** `cash.view`, with branch access (an ACCOUNTANT or owner sees all their branches). The preview needs no write rights, so an expired shop can still look at its days.
8. **Drift on closed days:** the detail read (route 4) answers the stored snapshot **and** `drift: boolean` = the live computation's totals differ from the stored ones. A closed day cannot take new rows, so drift means a bug or a manual database change; the job reports it as `DAY_DRIFT` (critical, decision 9).
9. **Nightly job `day.check`** (F13 registry, daily per shop at **03:30** shop time, after `ledger.reconcile` at 03:00): for every active branch, check **yesterday** and every **closed** day of the last 7 days. For yesterday: run the computation and upsert one `day_checks` row (`organization_id`, `branch_id`, `business_date`, `checked_at`, `issues jsonb`, `drift boolean`; UK (org, branch, date); RLS like `day_closes`, readable through the functions only). For a closed day: compare with the snapshot; drift → `DAY_DRIFT` in its `day_checks` row, logged at error level (Sentry, like `ledger.reconcile`). The job **never closes a day**. Audit `day.check_failed` (severity `critical` for `LEDGER_MISMATCH` / `DAY_DRIFT`, `warning` otherwise) only when there is something above `info`. The job succeeds even when it finds issues (retrying would not change them), as F13's first job.
10. **Dashboard alert:** `day_close_alerts()` (same readers as decision 7) answers, per branch the caller can see: `unclosed_days` (days with activity in the last 14 days that are not closed, oldest first, at most 14) and `last_check { business_date, warnings, critical }` from `day_checks`. The Web shows a card next to T03's ETA card when either is non-empty.
11. **Printing the close:** the closed day's page has "Print" (A4, Arabic, the snapshot only, `window.print()` with S04's print CSS). No print log (it is a report, not an issued document).

## Routes (5)
1. `GET /api/day-closes?branch_id&from&to&status` → `public.list_day_closes(...)`: days of the range (max 92 days) that have activity or a `day_closes` row, each `{ business_date, branch_id, status: 'open' | 'closed' | 'reopened', id?, closed_by, closed_at, sales_total, issues_count (from the row or the last day_checks) }`, newest first. Decision 7.
2. `GET /api/day-closes/preview?branch_id&date` → `public.get_day_close_preview(p_branch_id, p_date)`: `{ branch_id, business_date, status, totals, issues, hash, last_close? }` (decisions 1-4; `last_close` = the stored snapshot when the day is `reopened`). For a `closed` day it answers `DAY_ALREADY_CLOSED` with the row's id so the screen opens route 4.
3. `POST /api/day-closes` body `{ branch_id, business_date, preview_hash, note? }` (strict) → `public.close_day(...)`: decision 5. Answer the stored `DayClose`.
4. `GET /api/day-closes/:id` → `public.get_day_close(p_id)`: the stored row (`totals`, `issues`, who closed, the reopen fields) + `drift` (decision 8).
5. `POST /api/day-closes/:id/reopen` body `{ reason }` (strict) → `public.reopen_day(p_id, p_reason)`: decision 6.
Plus the dashboard read: `GET /api/day-closes/alerts` → `public.day_close_alerts()` counts as route 1's sibling: **if you add it as its own route the total is 278; say so in D84** (*default: add it*, it is cheap and the card needs it).

SQL tests (`supabase/tests/k04_day_close_test.sql`), at least:
- A day with cash sales, a split cash + bank sale, an installment sale with a down payment, two collections (one in the field), an expense, an expense reversal, a supplier payment, a vault-to-vault transfer, a float and a closed shift with a difference: every total equals the sum of that day's documents, computed independently in the test; each account's `closing − opening = in − out`; `total_closing_cash` equals the sum of the branch's safes, drawers and wallets.
- Another branch's and another shop's documents on the same day are not counted; a shop-wide bank shows only this branch's movements.
- An open shift → `SHIFT_OPEN` and `close_day` refuses with `DAY_CLOSE_BLOCKED`.
- A broken `cash_balances` row (disable the frozen trigger inside the test) → `LEDGER_MISMATCH`; a payment row inserted without its movement (same technique) → `DOCUMENT_WITHOUT_LEDGER` with the link to it.

- After the close: a sale, an expense, a collection, a transfer and a stock adjustment in that branch and day → `DAY_CLOSED`; the other branch can still post. Closing again → `DAY_ALREADY_CLOSED`.
- Reopen: BRANCH_MANAGER → `PERMISSION_DENIED`; owner with a reason → posting works again; close again → a new snapshot that includes the new entry; the audit log has `day.closed`, `day.reopened`, `day.closed`.
- Concurrency (pgbench or two sessions, like F10's parallel-sale proof): sales posting while the day is being closed either land before the close (and are in the snapshot) or fail with `DAY_CLOSED`; never a sale dated a closed day that is missing from its snapshot.

## Done when
- The day-close numbers equal the sum of the day's documents (SQL test computing each total independently, and the `DOCUMENT_WITHOUT_LEDGER` check is empty on a normal day).
- A broken balance is flagged: by the preview, in the stored issues, and by the nightly job (`day_checks` + audit + error log).
- A closed day refuses new entries for that branch (every dated ledger and document table), the owner can reopen it with a reason, and a re-close stores a new snapshot.
- Every shift closed is required; ETA receipts not accepted are listed with a link to the ETA monitor.
- Local checks: SQL tests, API unit + integration, Web unit + `tsc -b` + the new e2e, all green; D84, the data model's functions row "Day close (K04, D84)", `docs/testing/plans/K04.md` + the TEST_PLAN link.

## Out of scope
- Month-end close, P&L, stock valuation report (D-series reports), bank reconciliation against a bank statement.
- Auto-closing days (the job only checks), closing several branches at once.
- Corrections inside a closed day without reopening (V2), back-dated entry forms (M02 / V2).
- Card payments (V2), S05 returns themselves (K04 reads `sales_returns` and shows zeros until S05 lands), S10 order deposits (read when built; not in the totals now, say so in D84).
- Stock totals per day (stock movements are locked by the close through the baseline trigger, but not summarised here).

## Owner decisions (to confirm before the build)
1. An open shift **blocks** the close (`SHIFT_OPEN`), rather than closing it automatically or only warning.
2. ETA receipts not accepted are a **warning** (note required), not blocking.
3. A broken cached balance (`LEDGER_MISMATCH`) is a **warning** (note required), not blocking.
4. Days do **not** have to be closed in order; earlier open days are shown as `info`.
5. Closing **today** is allowed (the branch can't post again today until the owner reopens).
6. Reopening stays **owner only** (`day.reopen` as in the baseline role grants); a closed day is never edited, only reopened and closed again.
7. The nightly job runs at **03:30** shop time and never closes a day.
