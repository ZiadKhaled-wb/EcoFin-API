## Goal
A cashier prints the customer's receipt right after a sale, and anyone who may open a sale prints it again later, marked as a copy. The receipt is readable in Arabic or English on an 80 mm (or 58 mm) thermal roll and on A4, and carries the ETA receipt's UUID and a QR code a phone can scan.

- **Done when:** the client's printer prints a readable Arabic receipt with a scannable QR code.

## Decisions (record them in D68; the ones marked *default* are for the owner to confirm)
1. **Client only, like the labels (D45).** The receipt is drawn in the browser from the sale answer and printed with the browser's print dialog (a thermal printer, an A4 printer, or "Save as PDF"). No receipt is stored, no route, no audit row: printing changes nothing.
2. **No money arithmetic in the browser.** Every amount on the paper is a field of the sale answer, formatted with `formatMoney`. Nothing is added, multiplied or rounded. A value the answer doesn't carry is not printed.
3. **Paper.** Thermal at the shop's `receipt_width_mm` (80 or 58), and A4. The cashier picks thermal or A4 in the print dialog; the choice is remembered on the device (`localStorage`). *Default:* thermal first.
4. **Language.** The receipt has its own language, Arabic or English, picked in the print dialog. It starts at the shop's `default_language`, not at the user's screen language. The whole paper follows it (labels, direction, product names: `name_en` when English and present, else `name_ar`).
5. **"Copy" mark.** *Default:* the first print from the POS done card is the original. Every other print (a second press on the same done card, or any print from the sale page) carries a clear "COPY / نسخة" mark. This lives in the screen's state only: no print counter on the server, so a reprint is not audited. A strict count per sale would need a column and a route: a follow-up, not this task.
6. **QR code.** The repo has no QR encoder. *Default:* add **one** dependency, a small encoder with no dependencies of its own, pinned to an exact version and bundled by Vite (never a script tag, never a CDN, never an online QR image service: the payload must not leave the browser). Check the library with Context7 before you install it and name it, with its size in the build, in your summary. Error correction level M, black on white, a quiet zone of 4 modules, at least 25 mm wide on paper.
7. **ETA block.**
   - `eta` is `null` or absent → no ETA block at all.
   - `uuid` is set → print it (left to right, monospace, wrapped).
   - `qr_payload` is set and the status is `pending`, `submitted` or `accepted` → print the QR code.
   - `rejected`, `failed` or `cancelled` → no QR code. *Default:* the UUID is still printed.
   - `eta` is there but the payload is not built yet → Behaviour 6. *Default:* after a short wait the receipt can be printed without the QR code, and the QR code comes with a copy from the sale page.
8. **Other documents.** Returns (S05), customer payment receipts (N03) and contracts (N02) are not built yet. S04 builds the shared pieces (print root, shop header, footer, copy mark, QR code, the print dialog) so each of those slices adds only its own body. The supplier payment page keeps its own simple print.
9. **Not here:** opening the cash drawer, silent printing without the browser dialog, automatic printing after every sale, ESC/POS or any printer driver work. The client's real printer is tested in Q05.

## Behaviour
1. **Where printing starts.**
   - POS done card: a **Print receipt** button in the S04 slot, after **View**. Enter still starts the next sale; the focus stays on **New sale**.
   - Sale page (`/sales/:id`): a **Print receipt** button in the header actions.
   - On both, **Ctrl+P** (Cmd+P on a Mac) opens the same print dialog instead of the browser's own print of the screen (`preventDefault`). Add the listener only while a sale is shown and remove it on unmount. No other shortcut: the F keys are taken (D66).
2. **Print dialog.** A dialog (the project's `Dialog`) with:
   - paper: thermal (showing the shop's width) or A4;
   - language: Arabic or English;
   - a preview of the receipt as it will print, scaled to fit;
   - **Print** (has the focus; Enter prints) and **Close** (Esc).
   - **Print** calls `window.print()` once the logo and the QR code are drawn (or have failed). After the browser's dialog closes, the print dialog closes and the focus goes back to the button that opened it.
3. **What prints.** Only the receipt. Copy the C03 pattern: a `.print-root` portal in `document.body` and an `@page` rule for the chosen paper, added while the dialog is open and removed when it closes. The receipt root is always white with black text, whatever the app theme, and sets its own `dir` and `lang`.
   - Thermal: the content is 72 mm wide on 80 mm paper and 48 mm on 58 mm paper, margins 0, one continuous page (no forced page height, no page break inside a line). Check the result in Chrome's print preview and write in the test plan which `@page` rule you ended with and why.
   - A4: margins 12 mm, the lines table may run over pages, its header row repeats, a row never splits.
4. **Thermal layout, top to bottom.** Each part marked *(field)* shows only when its `receipt_fields` switch is on.
   - Logo *(logo)*, centred, at most 30 mm wide. No logo set, or the link fails → no logo and no gap; never block printing for it.
   - Shop `name`; `receipt_header` (keep its line breaks); `address` *(address)*; `phone` *(phone)*; `tax_registration_no` with its label *(tax_registration_no)*; the branch name *(branch)*.
   - The **COPY** mark when this print is a copy: large, on its own line, in both layouts.
   - Title ("Sale receipt"), the sale `number` (left to right), date and time from `issued_at` with `formatDateTime`, `shift_number`, cashier `created_by_name` *(cashier)*, `customer_name` or "Walk-in customer" *(customer)*.
   - Lines, in `line_no` order. Per line: the product name; under it the `serial` when there is one, else the `sku`; "Warranty until {date}" when `warranty_until` is set (`formatBusinessDate`); then `quantity` × `unit_price`, the line's `discount_amount` when above 0, and `line_total`.
   - Totals: `subtotal`; `discount_total` when above 0; `tax_total`; **`total`** (the largest text on the paper). Then the sentence that says whether prices include VAT (`prices_include_vat`; reuse the wording of `sales.detail.vatIncluded` / `vatAdded` in the receipt's language).
   - Payments: one row per payment with the method name and `amount`; for a bank transfer its `reference`; for cash `tendered` when set. Then **Change** with `change_total` when above 0.
   - ETA block (Decision 7): "ETA receipt", the UUID, the QR code centred.
   - `receipt_footer` (keep its line breaks).
5. **A4 layout.** The same data as an invoice: the shop block and logo at the top on one side, the document facts (title, number, date, shift, cashier, customer, the COPY mark) on the other; a table with the columns #, product (name, serial or SKU, warranty), quantity, unit price, discount, VAT, line total; the totals box; the payments table (payment number, method, account, reference, amount, received, change); the ETA block with the QR code; the footer. The same `receipt_fields` switches apply.
6. **ETA not ready yet.** When the dialog opens with `eta` set and `qr_payload` still `null` and a status of `pending`:
   - read the sale again with `salesApi.get(id)` every 2 seconds, at most 5 times, and stop when the payload arrives, the dialog closes or the page unmounts;
   - meanwhile the preview shows "Waiting for the ETA receipt…" in place of the QR code and **Print** stays enabled with the label "Print without QR code";
   - after the last try the note becomes "The ETA receipt is not ready. Print a copy later from the sale page to get the QR code." This note shows on screen only, never on the paper.
   - A done card whose sale was read again keeps the fresh answer, so a second print needs no new wait.
7. **Original and copy** (Decision 5). The done card remembers, for the sale it shows, that the receipt was printed (set when **Print** is pressed in the dialog). A new sale resets it. The sale page always prints a copy.
8. **Data for the receipt.** One hook gives the dialog everything: the sale (passed in), `settingsApi` settings, the branch name from `branchApi`, the logo link. Reuse the existing query keys so nothing is fetched twice. If the settings can't be read, show an error with **Retry** in the dialog and don't print a receipt without the shop block. If only the branch name or the logo fails, print without it.
9. **Long and odd values.** Names wrap, never cut. Serials, numbers, the UUID, phone numbers and references are left to right inside an Arabic receipt (`dir="ltr"`), amounts use the `num` style. A sale with 60 lines prints whole on both papers. Digits follow what `formatMoney` and `formatNumber` already give.
10. **Fonts.** The bundled IBM Plex Sans Arabic and IBM Plex Mono only (already dependencies). No font from the network. Thermal text is at least 2.6 mm high for body lines so it stays readable at 203 dpi.
11. **Text.** No hard-coded words. A new namespace `receipts` in Arabic and English with the same keys, registered where the others are (`src/i18n/resources.ts`). The receipt asks for its strings in the receipt's language, not the screen's (i18next's fixed-language `t`; don't switch the app language to print).
12. **Access.** No new permission and no route guard: whoever can see the sale can print it (the server already decides who reads a sale, D62). An expired, read-only shop can still print.
