## Goal
A shop that already sells on installments brings its **open installment debts** from its old system or paper records: each customer's open contracts with **what is left to pay**, line by line, the amounts paid before, what is already late, and the guarantors. It also brings the **opening cash** of each safe and bank. Both go through M01's screen: template, upload, dry run with every error by row and column, then an all-or-nothing import. Then the owner gets a **reconciliation report** (per customer and overall) and **signs it off** against the total from the old records. Done when the system's total open debt equals the signed total.

## Decisions (record them in D85)
1. **Two new import kinds in M01's pipeline:** `opening_contracts` and `opening_cash`. Same screen, same template download, same mapping step, same dry run, same job. `IMPORT_KINDS` gains both; `IMPORT_KIND_ORDER` puts them last (after `customers` and `opening_stock`). Required keys: `opening_contracts` = `import.data` + `installment.create` + `customer.create` (guarantors may be created); `opening_cash` = `import.data` + `cash.adjust` + `cash.account_manage` (missing accounts may be created). The installments module is required for `opening_contracts`.
2. **One row per remaining installment (one sheet, M01's reader unchanged).** Contract columns repeat on every row of that contract and are grouped by `contract_ref` (the old system's contract number, required, unique per shop among migrated contracts). Rows of one contract that disagree on a contract column → `IMPORT_CONTRACT_ROWS_DIFFER` on the later row. Columns (keys; Arabic and English labels and aliases in `IMPORT_COLUMNS`):
   - Contract: `contract_ref`*, `customer_national_id` or `customer_phone`* (one of them), `branch_code` (default: the main branch), `contract_date`* (the old signing date), `original_total`* (the installment price the customer signed for), `paid_before`* (everything paid before the migration, down payment included), `frequency` (`monthly` default, `weekly`, …), `remaining_principal` / `remaining_profit` (optional pair, decision 4), `notes`.
   - Line: `due_date`*, `amount`* (what is **left** on that installment; a line paid in part carries only its unpaid part).
   - Guarantors (optional, up to 2 per contract, on any row of the contract): `guarantor{1,2}_national_id`, `_name`, `_phone`, `_address`, `_relation`.
   - Rule checked per contract: `original_total - paid_before = sum(amount of its lines)` → else `IMPORT_CONTRACT_NOT_BALANCED` (row of the first line, with both numbers). Every amount > 0 with 2 decimals; dates `YYYY-MM-DD` or day first (M01's parser).
3. **What a migrated contract stores (the ledger stays clean):** the contract is written with **only what is left**: `financed_amount` = remaining principal, `markup_amount` = remaining profit, `total_payable` = their sum = the sum of the lines; `cash_price = financed_amount`, `down_payment = 0` (so the baseline CHECKs hold), `installments_count` = number of lines, `first_due_date` = the first line's date, `policy_id` = the current policy (the contract is **not** checked against it), `creditor` = the shop's current model, `is_migrated = true`, `migration_batch_id` = the import job, `status = 'active'`, a new EcoFin number (`app.next_document_number(..., 'contract')`), version-1 lines from the file (due date, principal, profit). New column `contracts.migration_source jsonb` (NULL for normal contracts): `{ contract_ref, contract_date, original_total, paid_before, notes }`; `contract_ref` is unique per shop (partial unique index on `(organization_id, (migration_source->>'contract_ref'))` where `is_migrated`). **Nothing is posted to the cash ledger, no payment, no stock movement:** the money paid before was received in the old system, and the goods left the shop long ago. The customer account (R02) and the contract page show "migrated from {contract_ref}, paid before: X" from `migration_source`.
4. **Principal and profit of what is left** (rule 3: the words are *installment price* and *profit*): if the row gives `remaining_principal` and `remaining_profit`, their sum must equal the lines' sum (`IMPORT_CONTRACT_NOT_BALANCED`); each line's split follows them in proportion, rounding on the last line (reuse `app.schedule_split`'s rounding rule). If the file gives neither, **everything left is principal and profit is 0** (recommended answer to owner question 2): exposure (R03) then counts it all, and nothing is ever shown as profit the shop can't prove.
5. **Overdue amounts are not a separate number:** a line whose due date is before the migration day and is unpaid **is** overdue; N04's nightly job treats it like any other (warnings, bans, legal referral after its days). Rule 4: **no fee** is ever added. The dry run reports per contract how much is already overdue and how many days, so the owner sees what N04 will do the next night.
6. **Customers and guarantors:** the customer must already exist (imported with M01 `customers` or typed in): matched by national ID, else by phone (M01's `app.import_customer_key` rules); none → `IMPORT_CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`; two → `IMPORT_AMBIGUOUS_MATCH`. A customer that is `banned` or archived is still accepted (the debt exists) and the dry run warns. Guarantors are matched by national ID (R04); a new one is created (needs name, national ID and phone, else `IMPORT_GUARANTOR_INCOMPLETE`); linked to the customer (`customer_guarantors`, relation) and to the contract (`contract_guarantors`). R04's "guarantor required above X" is not checked for migrated contracts (the trigger skips them).
7. **Re-import:** a `contract_ref` that already exists as a migrated contract → `IMPORT_CONTRACT_EXISTS` (no update: a migrated contract is a document; correct it with N05's tools after the import, or reverse the whole batch, decision 8). The same file twice therefore fails cleanly on every row.
8. **Undo a batch (before collections and sign-off only):** `reverse_migration_batch(job_id, reason)` (`import.data`, reason 3-300) is allowed while no contract of the batch has a collection, a contract change or an overdue action other than warnings (`MIGRATION_NOT_REVERSIBLE`, 409, with the first blocking contract) and while no sign-off covers the batch (`MIGRATION_SIGNED_OFF`). Contracts are never deleted: in one transaction each contract of the batch is closed through N05's write-off path (`contract_changes` kind `write_off`, status `written_off`, reason "Migration batch reversed: {reason}") and `migration_source.reversed = true`; the report leaves reversed contracts out. The batch's opening cash is not reversed here (use a K01 transfer). Audit `migration.batch_reversed` (warning). No new contract status (owner question 4).
9. **Opening cash (`opening_cash`):** one row per account: `branch_code`, `account_name`*, `kind`* (`safe` | `bank`), `bank_shop_wide` (bank only), `balance`* (≥ 0). An existing active safe or bank of that branch with that name is used; a missing one is created (K01 rules, audited as K01 does). Each balance is posted exactly like K01's `post_cash_opening` (one `opening` movement through this import job); an account that already has movements → `CASH_ACCOUNT_HAS_MOVEMENTS` row error (correct it with a K01 transfer or a K04 reopen + adjustment, not here). Balance 0 creates the account only. Drawers and collector wallets are not accepted (`IMPORT_ACCOUNT_KIND`).
10. **Business date:** every migrated contract and opening movement is dated **today** (the import day), like every other document; `contract_date` stays in `migration_source`. Today must not be closed (K04): a closed day fails the job with `DAY_CLOSED` (all or nothing).
11. **Reconciliation report:** `get_migration_report(filters)` (`import.data` or `report.financial`): per customer (name, national ID masked without `customer.view_pii`, number of migrated contracts, original total, paid before, **open now** = what is left on their migrated contracts today from `v_contract_balances`, overdue now, collected since migration), per branch, and overall; plus the opening cash per account (posted balance vs current balance); filter by batch (import job), branch, customer. Totals are computed by the server; amounts as strings. A CSV of the same report (M01's safe CSV writer: UTF-8 BOM, formula guard) for the owner to print and sign.
12. **Sign-off:** `sign_off_migration(body)` (`import.data`, owner by default) with `{ expected_open_total, expected_cash_total?, batch_ids[], note, signed_file_id? }`: the server computes the open total of those batches **at the moment of the import** (what was imported, not what is left after collections since) and compares; any difference → `MIGRATION_TOTAL_MISMATCH` (409, `{ expected, system, difference }`), nothing stored (Done when). Equal → one new `migration_signoffs` row (`id, organization_id, batch_ids uuid[], expected_open_total, system_open_total, expected_cash_total, system_cash_total, note, signed_file_id` (F14 purpose `other`, the scan of the signed paper), `signed_by, signed_at`), audit `migration.signed_off` (warning). A batch can be in one sign-off only (`MIGRATION_SIGNED_OFF`). After a sign-off its batches can't be reversed (decision 8).

## Done when
- The system's total open debt equals the signed total from the old records (sign-off refused otherwise), proven by the SQL test with a 30-line file and by the e2e.
- Nothing migrated touches the cash or stock ledgers except the opening cash movements; every migrated contract balances (`schedule_lines_sum_check`), carries `is_migrated`, its batch and its source; no fee anywhere.
- A bad file saves nothing; a re-import never duplicates; a batch can be reversed only before collections and sign-off.
- Local checks (SQL, API unit + integration, Web unit + `tsc -b` + e2e) green; D85, the data model rows (contracts `migration_source`, `migration_signoffs`, the functions row "Migration (M02, D85)"), `docs/testing/plans/M02.md` + TEST_PLAN link.

## Out of scope
- Migrating sales history, stock history, supplier balances or old receipts (only open installment debt and opening cash).
- Migrating contracts that are already finished (nothing left to pay) or written off in the old system.
- Late fees of the old system (rule 4: never carried over; the dry run says "fees in the old records are not imported").
- Reading the old system's database directly (only files through M01).

## Owner questions (recommended answer first; build with it unless told otherwise)
1. **File shape:** one row per remaining installment with the contract columns repeated (M01's one-sheet reader), *or* two sheets (contracts + installments). → **One row per installment.**
2. **Principal / profit of what is left when the old records don't split it:** everything left counts as principal (profit 0), *or* split by the original markup ratio. → **All principal** (nothing shown as profit that can't be proven; exposure is the safe side).
3. **Contract numbers:** a new EcoFin number with the old one kept as `contract_ref` (searchable), *or* keep the old number as the contract number. → **New number + `contract_ref`.**
4. **Undo a wrong batch:** close its contracts through the write-off path with a reason and leave them out of the report (no new status), *or* no undo at all (fix contract by contract). → **Write-off path, before any collection and before sign-off.**
5. **Sign-off strictness:** refuse any difference, *or* allow a difference with a written reason. → **Refuse any difference** (the slice's Done when).
6. **Who signs off:** `import.data` (owner by default), *or* owner only even if the key is granted to someone else. → **`import.data`.**
7. **Overdue lines on import day:** N04 treats them like any other the next night (warnings, bans by its rules), *or* N04 gives migrated contracts a grace of N days. → **Like any other** (the debt is real; no fee either way).
