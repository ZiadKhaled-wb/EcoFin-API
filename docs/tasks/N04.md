## Goal
Every day, in each shop's own time zone, the server looks at every open installment contract and, **without ever changing what anyone owes**: works out which installments are late (after the policy's grace days), puts a **warning** on the staff's work list the first day a contract is late and every N days after while it stays late, **blocks** a customer who is late more than the shop's ban days from new installment sales, and **flags a contract for legal referral** once it is late more than the shop's legal days for its frequency. Staff see one overdue list (days late, amounts, by branch) and work through the warnings; the owner dismisses or (later, L01) turns a legal flag into a case. Running the job twice in a day changes nothing. A policy can no longer carry a late fee.

## Owner decisions (17 Oct)
1. **Grace days:** an installment is **overdue only after due date + grace days**, where grace comes from the contract's own policy version (`contracts.policy_id → installment_policies.grace_days`). Once overdue, **days late is counted from the due date** (the honest number). Collections (N03), the customer account (R02) and aging follow automatically because they read the same view.
2. **Ban:** the job **blocks** the customer (R01 status `blocked`, with a reason) when any installment of any open contract is late **more than `customer_ban_days`**. The ban is **lifted only by hand** (R01's existing unblock, `customer.edit`). Blocked customers are already refused by every installment flow (R03, R04, R05, N01, N02: `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`); cash sales are not affected.
3. **Warnings:** the **first warning on the first day a contract is overdue**, then one **every `overdue_warning_interval_days`** while it stays overdue. Each warning is a row on a work list; staff mark it **handled with an outcome and a short note**. Sending reminders (SMS / WhatsApp) is N08.
4. **Legal referral:** recorded **once** when a contract crosses `legal_referral_days[frequency]`; it **stays on the list until it is handled**: the owner dismisses it with a reason, or L01 opens a legal case from it (the owner is building a Legal module with the LAWYER role: court cases and the lawyer; N04 only produces the referral). Paying part does not remove it.
5. **No late fees, ever:** the policy's late-fee fields are locked to `none` / 0 for every new policy version and removed from the policy screen; the unused `charges` table refuses new rows.

## Decisions (record them in D79)
1. **Overdue stays derived, not stored.** `v_schedule_line_status` already computes `status` and `days_late` live from due dates and payments, and N03 / R02 / R03 / aging read it. Recreate it (`CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW`, same columns, same order, so the dependent views stay) with one change: join the contract's policy and use `sl.due_date + p.grace_days < today` for `overdue`. A line past its due date but inside grace keeps status **`due`** (payable now, not late): **no new status value**, so no enum change reaches an older Web build. `days_late` stays `GREATEST(today − due_date, 0)` (consumers already read it only for `overdue` lines; check every reader and say so). A payment therefore clears "overdue" at once; the job never has to "un-mark" anything.
2. **The job only records events, under unique keys.** One table, `public.overdue_actions`, holds the three kinds of event the job creates; a unique key per kind makes a second run a no-op (`INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`). The job never writes `payments`, `schedule_lines`, `charges` or contract amounts.
3. **Table `public.overdue_actions`** (new): `id`, `organization_id`, `kind` (new enum `overdue_action_kind`: `warning`, `ban`, `legal_referral`), `contract_id`, `customer_id`, `branch_id` (the contract's), `schedule_line_id` (the contract's **oldest overdue line** when the event was made: the anchor), `business_date` (the job's shop date), `days_late`, `overdue_amount` (snapshots for the list), `status` (new enum `overdue_action_status`: `open`, `handled`, `superseded`, `resolved`, `dismissed`, `case_opened`), `outcome` (warnings: `contacted`, `promised`, `no_answer`, `wrong_number`, `other`), `note` (≤ 500), `handled_by`, `handled_at`, `legal_case_id` (nullable FK to `legal_cases`, for L01), `created_at`. Composite FKs with `organization_id` as everywhere. RLS: select for the shop's employees through the functions only (like N03's tables); no direct writes. Indexes: `(organization_id, kind, status)`, `(contract_id, kind, business_date DESC)`.
   - Unique keys: warning `(contract_id, business_date) WHERE kind = 'warning'`; ban `(schedule_line_id) WHERE kind = 'ban'`; legal referral `(schedule_line_id) WHERE kind = 'legal_referral'`; plus at most one **open** legal referral per contract `(contract_id) WHERE kind = 'legal_referral' AND status = 'open'`.
4. **Warning rule (owner decision 3), per contract, each run:** if the contract has an overdue line and **no warning with `business_date > today − N`** (N = `overdue_warning_interval_days`), or its latest warning is `resolved` (it caught up since), create a warning (`open`) and mark the contract's earlier `open` warnings `superseded`. If the contract has **no** overdue line, mark its `open` warnings `resolved`. A partial payment does not restart the cadence. A missed day is not back-filled (only today's warning is made).
5. **Ban rule (owner decision 2):** for every customer with an open contract whose oldest overdue line has `days_late > customer_ban_days` and no `ban` row for that line yet: insert the `ban` row (status `handled`, it is a record), and if the customer is `active`, set `blocked` + audit `customer.status_set` (severity `warning`, reason "Late more than {n} days on contract {number}", actor = none / system). Already blocked (by hand or before): the row is still written, no status change. After the owner unblocks, the same line never re-bans; a **later** line crossing the limit bans again. `customer_ban_days = 0` turns the ban off.
6. **Legal rule (owner decision 4):** for every open contract whose oldest overdue line has `days_late ≥ legal_referral_days[contract.frequency]` and no `legal_referral` row for that line, and no other `open` referral for the contract: insert one (`open`) + audit `contract.legal_referred`. It stays `open` until dismissed (`dismissed`, reason required, audit `contract.legal_referral_dismissed`) or L01 links a case (`case_opened`, not built here). A missing frequency key or 0 turns it off for that frequency.
7. **Which contracts:** status `active` or `rescheduled`, `is_migrated` included, current schedule version only (`superseded = false`). Completed, settled, written off and returned contracts are skipped (their open warnings become `resolved`).
8. **The job:** `'installments.overdue'`, daily at **00:30** shop time (before the day starts, so the morning list is ready). Handler calls `public.jobs_overdue_run(p_organization_id uuid, p_date date DEFAULT NULL)` (SECURITY DEFINER, `service_role` only, like `jobs_ledger_mismatches`); `p_date` defaults to `app.org_today(org)`, a past date is refused (the job only acts on today). Returns `{ warnings_created, warnings_resolved, bans, customers_blocked, legal_referrals }`; the handler logs it. Skip shops without the `installments` module or with an expired subscription. Replace the `installments.assess_late_fees` comment in `src/jobs/registry.ts`.
9. **Manual run for the owner:** `POST /api/overdue/run` → `public.run_overdue_check()`: OWNER only (`app.require_owner` or the role check N03 used), module `installments`, runs the same core for the caller's shop and today, same answer. Lets the owner see the effect on dev without waiting for 00:30. Twice in a row → second answer all zeros.
10. **Permissions (new, contracts 0.49.0):** `overdue.view` (see the overdue list, warnings and legal referrals) → OWNER, BRANCH_MANAGER, COLLECTOR, ACCOUNTANT, LAWYER (legal list only matters to them, the same key is enough); `overdue.handle` (mark a warning handled) → OWNER, BRANCH_MANAGER, COLLECTOR. Dismissing a legal referral: OWNER only. Branch rule: N03's `app.can_see_contract`, extended so holders of `overdue.view` see every branch like collectors do (one helper, say so in D79).
11. **Late-fee lock:** recreate `public.publish_installment_policy` so `late_fee_type` must be `none`, `late_fee_value` 0, `late_fee_recurring` false, `late_fee_cap` null; anything else → new error `LATE_FEES_NOT_ALLOWED` (422). The fields stay **optional** in the input (an older Web still sends `none` / 0 and must keep working); add `CHECK (late_fee_type = 'none' AND late_fee_value = 0 AND NOT late_fee_recurring AND late_fee_cap IS NULL) NOT VALID` on `installment_policies` (old versions stay readable; check dev and prod for a version with a fee first and report it). Trigger on `charges` refusing every INSERT (`LATE_FEES_NOT_ALLOWED`). The policy read keeps returning the fields (always none / 0) so nothing breaks; the Web stops showing them.
12. **Collector filter:** contracts have no assigned collector yet (N06). The list filters by **branch** now; the "by collector" filter is N06's. Say so in D79.

## Done when
- **Owed never changes:** for a seeded shop with late contracts, `v_contract_balances` (total, paid, remaining) and R02's account balance are identical before and after two job runs (SQL test asserts equality row by row).
- **Twice changes nothing:** second run of `jobs_overdue_run` on the same day returns all zeros and the `overdue_actions` / audit row counts are unchanged.
- **Grace:** grace 3, due 10th: on the 13th the line is `due` (not in the overdue list, no warning); on the 14th `overdue`, `days_late = 4`, first warning.
- **Warnings:** interval 10: warnings on overdue day 1, 11, 21; a partial payment on day 5 doesn't add one; paying everything → open warning `resolved`; late again later → a new warning that day.
- **Ban:** ban days 30: day 31 late → customer `blocked` + audit; N02 sale refused `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`; owner unblocks → next run doesn't re-block for the same line; a later line crossing → blocked again; already blocked by hand → row written, no second audit; ban days 0 → never.
- **Legal:** monthly 63: day 63 → one open referral; partial payment keeps it; dismiss (owner) with reason; non-owner dismiss → `PERMISSION_DENIED`; a later line crossing after a dismissal → a new referral.
- **Fee lock:** publishing a policy with `late_fee_type = 'fixed'` or value > 0 → `LATE_FEES_NOT_ALLOWED`, nothing written; with none / 0 or the fields omitted → published; an INSERT into `charges` is refused.
- **Isolation and access:** shop B's warning / referral ids → `OVERDUE_ACTION_NOT_FOUND`; the job for shop A never touches shop B; a cashier without `overdue.view` → `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
- All local checks green (unit, contracts, SQL files, integration, Web unit + e2e), as in N03.
