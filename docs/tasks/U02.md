## Goal
A warehouse keeper or manager records a supplier's invoice (lines, unit costs, VAT) as a **draft**. Someone with approval rights **approves** it. Approving puts every line into stock **once**, creates the serial units of serialized products, updates each product's **moving-average cost**, and raises what the shop owes the supplier by the invoice total. Or they **reject** it, with a reason.

## Slice text (U02)
- **Invoice:** supplier, branch, supplier's invoice number, date, lines (product, quantity, unit cost, tax), totals; optional link to a purchase order (U05).
- **States:** draft → approved (stock in via the ledger, once; product cost updated by moving average), or rejected.
- Serialized products require one serial per unit on approval.
- An invoice can be "pay later"; payment is a separate step (U03).
- Approval needs the right permission and follows the amount thresholds (U06).

**Done when:** approving a multi-line invoice adds each quantity exactly once; cost updates correctly; the supplier balance increases by the total.

## Decisions already made (record them in D54; don't change them)
1. **User decision:** unit costs are entered **before VAT**. VAT is added per line from the line's tax class: the product's class by default, changeable per line. The **moving-average cost uses the net (pre-VAT) unit cost**, because purchase VAT is recoverable input tax. The supplier balance increases by the **total including VAT**.
2. **Moving average is per product, across all branches:**
   - `new_avg = (old_qty × old_avg + qty × unit_cost) / (old_qty + qty)`, rounded half-up to **4 decimals**.
   - `old_qty` = the product's total `stock_levels.on_hand` (every branch, every stock state) **before** this line posts.
   - When `old_qty ≤ 0`, `new_avg = unit_cost`.
   - Lines post in `product_id` order. A product appears at most once per invoice.
3. **Approval is idempotent by state.** Approving an already approved invoice returns it unchanged: no new ledger rows, no audit row. This is the double-click guard. A rejected invoice can't be approved.
4. **Only drafts are editable.** The supplier and the branch are fixed at creation; to change them, reject the draft and create a new one.
5. **Tax is computed by the database** with `app.compute_document_tax` (D41), with `prices_include_vat = false` and no discounts. Draft totals are computed when saved. At approval, the lines are **recomputed with the current class rates** before posting; a rate change between draft and approval applies.
6. **No PO link in this task.** `po_id` and `po_line_id` stay NULL; U05 adds them. U06 adds the threshold. Until then, anyone with `purchase.invoice_approve` approves any amount (leave a SQL comment where U06 hooks in).
7. **The supplier invoice number** is optional. When given, it's unique per supplier among non-rejected invoices (baseline index). A clash → `SUPPLIER_INVOICE_NO_TAKEN`.

## Part A: server + contracts
1. **Migration** `20261011100000_u02_purchase_invoices.sql`. No new tables, columns or enums.
2. `app.purchase_invoice_json(p_org, p_id)` builds `PurchaseInvoice`: names, tax rate from the class, net = qty × unit_cost, `unit_ids` from `units.purchase_invoice_line_id`, outstanding from the view when approved. No grants.
3. `app.set_purchase_invoice_lines(p_org, p_invoice, p_lines jsonb)`: validates and replaces the lines of a **draft**, computes tax with `app.compute_document_tax`, writes `subtotal`, `tax_total`, `total`. Per line:
   - **Product:** must exist in the shop and not be archived (`PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`); a product twice → `VALIDATION_FAILED: duplicate product`.
   - `quantity` 1–100 000 and `unit_cost` ≥ 0 with ≤ 2 decimals (`VALIDATION_FAILED`).
   - **Tax class:** `tax_class_id` defaults to the product's class; it must be the shop's and active (`TAX_CLASS_NOT_FOUND`).
   - **Serials:**
     - on a non-serialized product → `UNIT_NOT_ALLOWED`;
     - on a serialized product, optional on a draft, but when given they must parse (`app.parse_serials`), number exactly `quantity` (`VALIDATION_FAILED`), and not repeat across the invoice (`SERIAL_TAKEN`);
     - a serialized line needs the `serials` module (`MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN`).
4. `public.create_purchase_invoice(p_supplier_id, p_branch_id, p_supplier_invoice_no, p_invoice_date, p_lines jsonb, p_idempotency_key)`, `purchase.invoice_create`:
   - `app.lock_idempotency_key`; the same key again → the same invoice, no new row.
   - The supplier must be the shop's (`SUPPLIER_NOT_FOUND`) and active (`SUPPLIER_ARCHIVED`).
   - The branch: `require_branch_access`.
   - A date in the future (shop date) → `VALIDATION_FAILED: date`.
   - Number from `next_document_number(org, branch, 'purchase_invoice')`.
   - Lines via the helper. Status `draft`. Audit `purchase_invoice.created`.
5. `public.update_purchase_invoice(p_id, p_changes jsonb)`, `purchase.invoice_create`:
   - Lock; another shop's or missing → `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND`.
   - The caller needs the invoice's branch.
   - Not draft → `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_EDITABLE`.
   - Allowed keys: `supplier_invoice_no`, `invoice_date`, `lines`.
   - One audit row with what changed; no change → no row.
6. `public.approve_purchase_invoice(p_id)`, `purchase.invoice_approve`:
   1. Lock the invoice. Already `approved` → return it (decision 3). `rejected` → `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_EDITABLE`.
   2. The caller needs the branch (`require_branch_access`: inactive → `BRANCH_INACTIVE`). The supplier must still be active (`SUPPLIER_ARCHIVED`).
   3. Every serialized line must have its serials → else `SERIALS_REQUIRED` with the line ids (nothing posted).
   4. Recompute the lines and totals with the current rates (decision 5).
   5. For each line in `product_id` order:
      - lock the product;
      - compute the new average (decision 2) and update `avg_cost`;
      - then post the stock: `app.create_units(...)` for serialized lines (it inserts the movements), or one `stock_movements` row for the others.
   6. Set `status='approved', approved_by, approved_at`. Audit `purchase_invoice.approved` with `{total, lines, avg_cost before/after per product}`.
   7. Any error rolls back everything: a taken serial means no stock, no cost change, still a draft.
   8. Leave the comment `-- U06: threshold check goes here` before step 5.
7. `public.reject_purchase_invoice(p_id, p_reason)`, `purchase.invoice_approve`:
   - `draft` or `pending_approval` → `rejected` with `rejected_reason` (trim, 3–500; else `VALIDATION_FAILED`).
   - Already rejected or approved → `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_EDITABLE`.
   - Audit `purchase_invoice.rejected` (severity `warning`, reason).
8. **Reads** (`app.can_read_suppliers()`, **only the caller's branches**: another branch's invoice → `PURCHASE_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND`):
