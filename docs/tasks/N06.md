## Goal
A collector visits customers and takes installment payments in the field. The cash they take goes into **their own wallet** (a cash account with its own ledger), never straight into a vault, so it is counted once: in the wallet until they hand it in. Handing it in (a **settlement**) moves it from the wallet to a safe in one transaction; anything missing is recorded as a **shortage** with a reason. Each field collection earns the collector a **commission** (the shop's percentage), kept apart as money the shop owes the collector and paid later as an expense; it is never added to the wallet. Managers assign customers to collectors; a collector sees their own customers and a **daily route** of who owes something today.

## Decisions (record them in D81)
1. **The wallet is a cash account.** `cash_accounts` row, `kind = 'collector_wallet'`, `employee_id` = the collector, `branch_id` = the collector's default branch (where their cash is handed in and where the day is closed), name "Wallet · {employee name}". One per collector (baseline unique index). It is **created on the collector's first field collection**, in the same transaction (no separate setup step). K01 keeps not listing or managing wallets. Its balance is `cash_balances` like every account; the ledger trigger already refuses a wallet below 0.
2. **No new key, no new enum.** Field collection uses `collection.create`; settlement and commission payout use `collector.settle` (OWNER always, BRANCH_MANAGER and ACCOUNTANT by default); assigning customers uses `customer.edit`. The types `collector_wallet`, `collector_settlement`, `commission_status` and the movement types `collection`, `settlement`, `adjustment` all exist in the baseline. An older Web build parses everything.
3. **Assignment (new column, the only schema change besides the commission link):** `customers.collector_id uuid NULL` (composite FK to `employees`), index `(organization_id, collector_id)`. The collector must be an active employee of the shop holding `collection.create` (else `COLLECTOR_INVALID`, 422). Assign / change / clear through `set_customer_collector(customer, employee | null, reason?)`, audit `customer.collector_set` with before / after. One collector per customer (all their contracts). History lives in the audit log.
4. **Field collection** = N03's collection in the field: `post_field_collection(body, key)`, same body as `POST /api/collections` (customer, contracts with amounts and `expected_remaining`, payment lines). Rules: `collection.create`, module `installments`; **no shift and no POS device** (it is not a POS write; the device rule doesn't apply); the customer must be **assigned to the caller** (`CUSTOMER_NOT_ASSIGNED`, 403), unless the caller holds `collector.settle` (a manager collecting on someone's behalf still books into **their own** wallet). Cash lines go to the caller's wallet (`collection` movement, `shift_id` NULL, branch = the wallet's branch); bank-transfer lines go to the bank account as in N03. It calls `app.post_collection_core(org, emp, wallet.branch_id, NULL, wallet.id, body, key)`; allocation, completion, receipt, idempotency and audit `collection.created` are N03's, unchanged. The receipt shows "Collected in the field by {name}".
5. **Commission:** on every field collection, one `collector_commissions` row for the receipt (`payment_id` = the receipt row, unique): `amount` = round half-up to the piaster of `org_settings.collector_commission_pct` % × **the whole collection amount** (cash + bank), read at the time of the collection; nothing when the percentage is 0. Status `accrued`. It is never a cash movement and never touches the wallet. Collections at the counter (N03, with a shift) earn none.
6. **Reversing a field collection:** `reverse_collection` (recreated) accepts a receipt without a shift when its cash went to a wallet: same business day, the latest collection of its contracts (N03's rules), N05's guard, and the money goes back out of the **same wallet** (the ledger refuses it if the wallet no longer holds it: `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` → shown as "already handed in"). Its commission becomes `cancelled`. Counter collections keep N03's shift rule.
7. **Settlement** (`collector.settle`, branch access to the wallet's branch, module `installments`): `settle_collector_wallet(wallet_id, body, key)`, body `{ to_account_id, handed, shortage?, reason?, expected_balance }`. `to_account_id` is an active **safe** of the wallet's branch (`CASH_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` otherwise). `expected_balance` must equal the wallet's balance now (`WALLET_BALANCE_CHANGED`, 409, `{balance}`). `handed + shortage ≤ balance`; a **partial** settlement is allowed (the collector keeps the rest); `shortage > 0` needs a reason (3-300). One transaction: a `cash_transfers` row (`kind = 'collector_settlement'`, `amount = handed`, `shortage_amount`, `reason`, number CX), `transfer_out` from the wallet and `transfer_in` to the safe for `handed`, and, for a shortage, one `adjustment` movement of `−shortage` on the wallet (source the same transfer). Audit `collector.wallet_settled` (severity `warning` with a shortage). The caller can't settle their own wallet (`SELF_SETTLEMENT`, 403). Idempotent by key; accounts locked in id order like K01. `handed = 0` with a shortage only is allowed (cash lost).
8. **No surplus:** handing in more than the wallet holds is refused (the body can't express it); extra cash means an unrecorded collection, which is recorded as a field collection first.
9. **Paying commissions:** `pay_collector_commissions(employee_id, body, key)` (`collector.settle`), body `{ cash_account_id, up_to?: date }`: every `accrued` commission of that collector (up to the date, default today) is paid as **one K03 expense** (category "Collector commissions", created on first use; from a safe or bank the caller can use; normal expense rules and audit `expense.created`), and those rows become `paid` with `paid_by_expense_id` and `paid_at`, in one transaction. Nothing accrued → `NOTHING_TO_PAY` (409). A shortage is **not** deducted automatically (see To confirm).
10. **Reads:**
    - **My wallet** (any holder of `collection.create`; the caller's own; no wallet yet → zeros): balance, today's field collections (count, amount), accrued commission, last settlement.
    - **Wallets** (`collector.settle` or `cash.view`, the caller's branches; OWNER all): one row per wallet with balance, oldest unsettled collection date, accrued commission.
    - **A wallet's ledger** (same keys, or the collector's own): movements newest first with a running balance (K01's `list_cash_account_movements` shape) and its settlements with shortages.
    - **Commissions** (`collector.settle`; a collector sees their own): filter by collector, status, dates; totals.
    - **Route** (`collection.create`; a collector only their own, `collector.settle` any collector): for a date (default today), the collector's assigned customers with an open contract that has something **due or overdue on that date** (N04's view: `due`, `partly_paid` with a due date ≤ the date, `overdue`), with phone, address, city, amount due, amount overdue, max days late, next due date; sorted by city, address, name.
11. **N04 collector filter (D79 left it here):** `list_overdue_contracts` gains the filter `collector_id` and each row's `customer.collector_id`. A COLLECTOR caller (holds `collection.create` and `overdue.view` but not `collector.settle`) sees only their assigned customers' rows.
12. **Wallets in K04 (day close, not built yet):** a wallet's movements carry its branch and business date like every account, so K04 will count them; nothing to do here, say so in D81.

## Done when
- **Counted once:** after a field collection of 1 000 in cash, the wallet is +1 000, no drawer, safe or vault moved, and the contract's paid is +1 000; after settling it the wallet is 0 and the safe +1 000 (+ handed only, with a shortage). The sum of every account's balance changes by exactly the collected cash, never twice (SQL test over `cash_balances` before / after).
- **Wallet = its ledger:** for every wallet, `cash_balances.balance` = sum of its movements (the ledger invariant check covers wallets).
- **Settlement:** atomic (a failure mid-way leaves nothing), partial allowed, shortage needs a reason, stale `expected_balance` refused, self-settlement refused, twice with one key = one transfer.
- **Commission:** 2 % of a 1 000 collection = 20.00 accrued, never a cash movement, never in the wallet; a counter collection earns none; reversing the field collection cancels it; paying writes one expense for the sum and marks the rows paid; nothing accrued → `NOTHING_TO_PAY`.
- **Assignment and route:** a collector can't field-collect for a customer not assigned to them; the route shows only their customers with something due or overdue on the date, sorted by city / address; the overdue list filters by collector and a collector sees only theirs.
- **Reversal:** a same-day field collection still in the wallet reverses (wallet back down, contract unpaid, commission cancelled); after the cash was handed in it is refused.
- **Isolation and access:** shop B's wallet / commission / customer ids → not found; a cashier can't settle or pay; a collector can't read another collector's wallet.
- All local checks green (unit, contracts, SQL files, integration, Web unit + e2e), as in N05.

## Out of scope
GPS or proof of visit, offline field collection (the phone must be online), printed field receipts on a mobile printer, collector targets or performance reports (D0x), deducting shortages from commissions automatically, collectors' salaries, guarantor follow-up visits (L01), late fees of any kind (never: rule 4).

## To confirm (defaults used unless the owner says otherwise)
1. **Commission base:** the whole field collection amount (cash and bank transfer). Alternative: cash only.
2. **Who may field-collect for a customer:** only the assigned collector, or anyone holding `collector.settle` (into their own wallet). Alternative: any holder of `collection.create`.
3. **Assignment granularity:** one collector per **customer** (all their contracts). Alternative: per contract.
4. **Shortage:** recorded on the settlement with a reason only (no automatic deduction from commission, no debt on the employee). Alternative: deduct it from the next commission payout.
5. **Partial settlement allowed** (the collector keeps the rest for tomorrow). Alternative: always settle the whole balance.
6. **Wallet created automatically** on the first field collection, booked to the collector's default branch. Alternative: a manager creates each wallet first.
