## Goal
A collector visits customers and takes installment payments in the field. The cash they take goes into **their own wallet** (a cash account with its own ledger), never straight into a vault, so it is counted once: in the wallet until they hand it in. Handing it in (a **settlement**) moves it from the wallet to a safe in one transaction; anything missing is recorded as a **shortage** with a reason. Managers assign customers to collectors; a collector sees their own customers and a **daily route** of who owes something today. Collectors are paid a salary: **there is no collection commission**.

## Owner decisions (18 Oct)
1. **No commission.** Collectors have their own salary; the system calculates and pays no commission of any kind. The baseline `collector_commissions` table and `org_settings.collector_commission_pct` stay **unused** (like `charges` in N04): the migration sets the percentage to 0 for every shop, the Web settings screen stops showing it, nothing reads it. The slice line "collector commission calculated separately" is removed (`docs/EcoTech_Pro_Slices.md`, Jira EC-85).
2. **Who may field-collect:** the customer's **assigned collector**, or anyone holding `collector.settle` (a manager); the cash always goes into the collecting person's own wallet.
3. **Assignment per customer:** one collector covers all of a customer's contracts.
4. **Shortage: recorded only:** on the settlement, with a reason. No automatic deduction and no debt booked on the employee.
5. **Partial settlement allowed:** the collector may hand in part and keep the rest for the next day.
6. **Wallets are automatic:** created on the collector's first field collection, under their default branch.

## Decisions (record them in D81)
1. **The wallet is a cash account.** `cash_accounts` row, `kind = 'collector_wallet'`, `employee_id` = the collector, `branch_id` = the collector's default branch (where their cash is handed in and where the day is closed), name "Wallet · {employee name}". One per collector (baseline unique index), created on the first field collection in the same transaction (owner decision 6). K01 keeps not listing or managing wallets. Its balance is `cash_balances` like every account; the ledger trigger already refuses a wallet below 0.
2. **No new key, no new enum.** Field collection uses `collection.create`; settlement uses `collector.settle` (OWNER always, BRANCH_MANAGER and ACCOUNTANT by default); assigning customers uses `customer.edit`. `collector_wallet`, `collector_settlement` and the movement types exist in the baseline. An older Web build parses everything.
3. **Assignment (the only schema change):** `customers.collector_id uuid NULL` (composite FK to `employees`), index `(organization_id, collector_id)`. The collector must be an active employee of the shop holding `collection.create` (else `COLLECTOR_INVALID`, 422). Assign / change / clear through `set_customer_collector(customer, employee | null, reason?)`, audit `customer.collector_set` with before / after. History lives in the audit log.
4. **Field collection** = N03's collection in the field: `post_field_collection(body, key)`, same body as `POST /api/collections` (customer, contracts with amounts and `expected_remaining`, payment lines). Rules: `collection.create`, module `installments`; **no shift and no POS device** (not a POS write); the customer must be **assigned to the caller** (`CUSTOMER_NOT_ASSIGNED`, 403) unless the caller holds `collector.settle` (owner decision 2). Cash lines go to the caller's wallet (`collection` movement, `shift_id` NULL, branch = the wallet's branch); bank-transfer lines go to the bank account as in N03. It calls `app.post_collection_core(org, emp, wallet.branch_id, NULL, wallet.id, body, key)`; allocation, completion, receipt, idempotency and audit `collection.created` are N03's, unchanged. The receipt shows "Collected in the field by {name}".
5. **Reversing a field collection:** `reverse_collection` (recreated) accepts a receipt without a shift when its cash went to a wallet: same business day, the latest collection of its contracts (N03's rules), N05's guard, and the money goes back out of the **same wallet** (the ledger refuses it if the wallet no longer holds it: `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, shown as "already handed in"). Counter collections keep N03's shift rule.
6. **Settlement** (`collector.settle`, branch access to the wallet's branch, module `installments`): `settle_collector_wallet(wallet_id, body, key)`, body `{ to_account_id, handed, shortage?, reason?, expected_balance }`. `to_account_id` is an active **safe** of the wallet's branch (`CASH_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` otherwise). `expected_balance` must equal the wallet's balance now (`WALLET_BALANCE_CHANGED`, 409, `{balance}`). `handed > 0`, `handed + shortage ≤ balance` (partial allowed, owner decision 5); `shortage > 0` needs a reason (3-300). One transaction: a `cash_transfers` row (`kind = 'collector_settlement'`, `amount = handed`, `shortage_amount`, `reason`, number CX in the wallet's branch), `transfer_out` from the wallet and `transfer_in` to the safe for `handed`, and, for a shortage, one `adjustment` movement of `−shortage` on the wallet (source the same transfer; owner decision 4: recorded only). A shortage always comes with a settlement that hands something in (`cash_transfers.amount > 0` in the baseline, and no doc type exists for a stand-alone wallet adjustment); a wallet whose cash is all lost is out of scope for N06. Audit `collector.wallet_settled` (severity `warning` with a shortage). The caller can't settle their own wallet (`SELF_SETTLEMENT`, 403). Idempotent by key; accounts locked in id order like K01.
7. **No surplus:** handing in more than the wallet holds can't be expressed; extra cash means an unrecorded collection, which is recorded as a field collection first.
8. **Reads:**
   - **My wallet** (any holder of `collection.create`; the caller's own; no wallet yet → zeros): balance, today's field collections (count, amount), the oldest unsettled collection date, the last settlement.
   - **Wallets** (`collector.settle` or `cash.view`, the caller's branches; OWNER all): one row per wallet with balance, oldest unsettled collection date, last settlement.
   - **A wallet's ledger** (same keys, or the collector's own): movements newest first with a running balance (K01's `list_cash_account_movements` shape), settlements with their shortages.
   - **Route** (`collection.create`; a collector only their own, `collector.settle` any collector): for a date (default today), the collector's assigned customers with an open contract that has something **due or overdue on that date** (N04's view: `due`, `partly_paid` with a due date ≤ the date, `overdue`), with phone, address, city, amount due, amount overdue, max days late, next due date; sorted by city, address, name.
9. **N04 collector filter (D79 left it here):** `list_overdue_contracts` gains the filter `collector_id` and each row's `customer.collector_id`. A collector (holds `collection.create` and `overdue.view` but not `collector.settle`) sees only their assigned customers' rows.
10. **Commission setting retired (owner decision 1):** the migration sets `org_settings.collector_commission_pct = 0` for every shop; `update_settings` keeps accepting the field (an older Web sends it) but nothing reads it; the contracts mark it deprecated. No commission rows are ever written.
11. **Wallets in K04 (day close, not built yet):** a wallet's movements carry its branch and business date like every account, so K04 will count them; nothing to do here, say so in D81.

## Done when
- **Counted once:** after a field collection of 1 000 in cash, the wallet is +1 000, no drawer, safe or vault moved, and the contract's paid is +1 000; after settling it the wallet is down and the safe up by the handed amount only. The sum of every account's balance changes by exactly the collected cash minus any shortage, never twice (SQL test over `cash_balances` before / after).
- **Wallet = its ledger:** for every wallet, `cash_balances.balance` = sum of its movements (the ledger invariant check covers wallets).
- **Settlement:** atomic, partial allowed, shortage needs a reason, stale `expected_balance` refused, self-settlement refused, twice with one key = one settlement, more than the balance refused.
- **No commission:** no `collector_commissions` row is ever written; every shop's `collector_commission_pct` is 0; the settings screen has no commission field.
- **Assignment and route:** a collector can't field-collect for a customer not assigned to them (a manager can, into their own wallet); the route shows only their customers with something due or overdue on the date, sorted by city / address; the overdue list filters by collector and a collector sees only theirs.
- **Reversal:** a same-day field collection still in the wallet reverses (wallet back down, contract unpaid); after the cash was handed in it is refused.
- **Isolation and access:** shop B's wallet / customer ids → not found; a cashier can't settle; a collector can't read another collector's wallet.
- All local checks green (unit, contracts, SQL files, integration, Web unit + e2e), as in N05.

## Out of scope
Commissions of any kind (owner decision 1: collectors are salaried), salaries and payroll, GPS or proof of visit, offline field collection (the phone must be online), printed field receipts on a mobile printer, collector targets or performance reports (D0x), deducting shortages from anything (owner decision 4), a settlement that hands nothing in (all the cash lost), guarantor follow-up visits (L01), late fees of any kind (never: rule 4).
