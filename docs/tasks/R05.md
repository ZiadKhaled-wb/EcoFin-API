## Goal
Before the shop lends its own money above a set amount, someone visits the customer's home or work. A manager assigns the visit to an employee (often a collector); the employee opens it on a phone, answers a checklist, scores six criteria, writes notes, captures the GPS location and takes photos, then submits. A manager approves or rejects it. The latest approved survey feeds the **residence** part of the R03 credit score and shows on the credit profile. The installment policy says above which financed amount a contract needs an approved survey, and the server refuses such a contract without one, even when the client calls the database directly.

## Slice text (R05)
- **Includes:** assign a survey to an employee; the employee fills a checklist, scores, notes, GPS location and photos from a phone; manager approves or rejects; the result shows on the credit profile (R03); settings can require an approved survey above an amount.
- **Done when:** photos are private (F14); a required survey blocks the installment sale until approved.

## Decisions (record them in D75; the ones marked *to confirm* are my defaults: post them on EC-67 for the owner before Part B)
1. **Flow:** `assigned` (created by a manager) → `submitted` (by the assignee) → `approved` | `rejected` (by a decider). `cancelled` from `assigned` or `submitted`. Approved / rejected / cancelled are final (baseline freeze trigger). A rejected or cancelled survey is history; a new visit is a new survey.
2. **Columns added to `field_surveys`** (the only table change; no enum, CHECK or permission change):
   - `visit_type text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'home' CHECK (visit_type IN ('home', 'work'))`;
   - `checklist jsonb NOT NULL DEFAULT '{}'::jsonb` (answers, decision 4);
   - `due_date date` (optional, set by the manager);
   - `instructions text` (≤ 500, from the manager: "ask for the electricity bill"…);
   - `survey_score smallint CHECK (survey_score BETWEEN 0 AND 100)` (written on submit, decision 5);
   - `decision_note text` (the decider's note, or the cancel reason);
   - `client_captured_at timestamptz` (when the phone took the GPS fix; shown to the reviewer).
   Existing columns: `scores` holds the six criteria, `notes` the employee's notes, `lat`/`lng`/`gps_accuracy_m` the fix. Adding columns to a table with a BEFORE UPDATE trigger is fine (DDL).
3. **Criteria** (`scores`, all required on submit, integers 1-10): `housing_quality` (جودة السكن), `neighbourhood` (جودة الحي), `income_stability` (استقرار الدخل), `job_status` (الحالة الوظيفية), `property_ownership` (ملكية العقار), `overall_impression` (الانطباع العام). Unknown keys → `VALIDATION_FAILED: scores`.
4. **Checklist** (`checklist`, every item answered `true`/`false` on submit; *to confirm* the list): `met_customer` (met the customer in person), `address_matches` (the address matches the file), `id_original_seen` (saw the original national ID), `neighbours_confirm` (neighbours confirm the customer lives / works there), `utility_bill_seen` (saw a recent utility bill in the customer's name), `workplace_confirmed` (the employer / workplace confirms the job). Unknown keys → `VALIDATION_FAILED: checklist`.
5. **Survey score** (0-100, integer, half-up) = `round(avg(criteria) × 10)`, stored in `survey_score` on submit. Equal weights (*to confirm*; the old `SCORE_WEIGHTS` are not ported). The checklist doesn't enter the score; the reviewer sees it.
6. **R03 residence component:** `value` = latest **approved** and **valid** survey's `survey_score / 100`, else 0. **Valid** = decided within the last **180 days** (`app.survey_valid_days()` = 180, a constant function like R03's TTL; *to confirm*). Re-create `app.customer_credit_score` from its **latest** version (R03) with only the residence line changed and the `TODO(R05)` removed.
7. **GPS:** required on submit: `lat` (-90..90), `lng` (-180..180), `gps_accuracy_m` (≥ 0), `client_captured_at` (not more than 5 minutes in the future of the server clock, not before the survey was created). The server stores; it doesn't judge. The answer carries `gps_low_accuracy = gps_accuracy_m > 100` (*to confirm*) and the reviewer page shows a warning. No distance check (customers have no coordinates: out of scope).
8. **Photos = F14 files**, purpose `survey_photo`, **entity type `field_survey`** (new: add `field_survey: 'field_surveys'` to `ENTITY_TABLES` in `src/services/files.rules.ts` and `WHEN 'field_survey' THEN 'field_surveys'` to `public.register_file`; re-create the function from its **latest** version (R04, `20261015090000_r04_guarantors.sql`)). In `register_file`, for `p_entity_type = 'field_survey'` (copy the `warranty_claim` block's shape): entity id required, purpose must be `survey_photo`, lock the survey `FOR UPDATE`, status must be `assigned` (`SURVEY_WRONG_STATUS`), the caller must be the assignee (`SURVEY_NOT_ASSIGNEE`), at most **10** photos per survey (`VALIDATION_FAILED: at most ten survey photos`). Upload key `survey.submit` and read key `customer.view_pii` stay as they are: photos are private (signed links only), and the visiting collector who has no `customer.view_pii` can't open them afterwards (*to confirm*; the phone shows its own local previews before upload). Submit needs **at least 1** photo → `SURVEY_PHOTO_REQUIRED`. Photos are never deleted.
9. **Who (no new permission keys; `permissions.ts` must keep equalling the baseline seed):**
   - **Assign, reassign, cancel:** `survey.assign`.
   - **Save answers, submit:** `survey.submit` **and** be the survey's `assigned_to` (`SURVEY_NOT_ASSIGNEE`, 403). An owner who wants to fill it reassigns it to themselves.
   - **Decide:** `survey.decide`; the assignee can't decide their own survey unless the decider is the OWNER (`SURVEY_SELF_DECISION`; same rule as R03 exceptions, *to confirm*).
   - **Read:** the R01 read guard `app.customer_read_guard()`. A caller with `survey.submit` but neither `survey.assign` nor `survey.decide` (the collector) sees **only surveys assigned to them**; any other survey is `SURVEY_NOT_FOUND` for them (list filtered, detail 404).
   - Every write: plan module `customers`; an expired shop can read but not write.
10. **Assign rules:** the customer must exist in the shop (`CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`) and be `active` or `watch` (blocked or archived → `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`); the assignee must be an **active employee of the shop who has `survey.submit`** (`SURVEY_ASSIGNEE_INVALID`, with `{ employee_id }`); at most **one open survey** (`assigned` or `submitted`) per customer → `SURVEY_ALREADY_OPEN` with `{ survey_id, status }`. `due_date` not in the past (shop timezone, `app.org_today`). **Reassign** (status `assigned` only): the same assignee checks; answers saved so far are kept; already uploaded photos stay. **Cancel**: reason 3-300 required (stored in `decision_note`).
11. **Save answers** (status `assigned`, the assignee): any subset of `visit_type`, `scores`, `checklist`, `notes` (≤ 2000), `lat`/`lng`/`gps_accuracy_m`/`client_captured_at` (all four together or none); partial `scores`/`checklist` allowed here (each given value still validated); merges into what is saved (a key sent as `null` clears it). Same values again = no change, no audit row. **Submit**: the same body shape, merged, then everything in decisions 3, 4, 7 and the photo rule is required; writes `survey_score`, `submitted_at`, status `submitted`.
12. **Decide:** status `submitted` only (`SURVEY_WRONG_STATUS` otherwise); `decision` `approve` | `reject`; a rejection needs a note (3-500, `VALIDATION_FAILED: note`), an approval may have one; writes `decided_by/at`, `decision_note`, status. A customer blocked meanwhile can still be decided (the survey is a fact about the visit).
13. **Settings: survey above an amount.** `installment_policies.survey_required_above` (exists; NULL = never). When the contract's **financed amount** (`cash_price − down_payment`, same as R04) is **greater than** it, the contract needs a valid approved survey (decision 6) of that customer.
14. **Contract rules (for N01/N02; no route of their own; not granted to `authenticated`):**
    - `app.survey_required(p_org uuid, p_policy_id uuid, p_financed_amount numeric) RETURNS boolean`.
    - `app.latest_valid_survey(p_org uuid, p_customer_id uuid) RETURNS uuid` (the newest approved survey decided within 180 days, or NULL).
    - `app.check_contract_survey(p_org uuid, p_customer_id uuid, p_policy_id uuid, p_financed_amount numeric) RETURNS uuid` — returns the survey id used (NULL when not required); raises `SURVEY_REQUIRED` with `{ required_above, financed_amount, open_survey_id, open_status, last_decided_status }` in the details.
    - **Safety net:** `CONSTRAINT TRIGGER trg_contracts_survey_check AFTER INSERT ON public.contracts DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED FOR EACH ROW` that at commit calls the check; skipped for `is_migrated` contracts. This makes "a required survey blocks the installment sale until approved" true for any writer.
15. **Audit actions** (entity type `field_survey`, `after` with customer id and assignee; never the GPS point in `before/after` of reads): `survey.assigned`, `survey.reassigned` (before/after assignee), `survey.cancelled` (`warning`), `survey.saved` (changed keys only), `survey.submitted` (with `survey_score`), `survey.approved`, `survey.rejected` (`warning`).
