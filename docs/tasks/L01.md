## Goal
The owner turns a defaulted contract into a **legal case**: from N04's legal referral list or straight from the contract page. The case carries the court details, the assigned lawyer, its status (preparing → filed → in court → judgment → closed), hearings, notes and documents. While a case is open the contract and the customer account show a **"legal" flag** (this is N09). A `LAWYER` employee sees **only the cases assigned to them**: no sales, no cash, no other screens. Money received through the legal process is taken as a normal collection (N03 at the counter, or N06 by a collector); the case shows what was paid since it was opened. Nothing in this task ever changes what the customer owes.

## Owner defaults (proposed 24 Oct, build with them unless the owner says otherwise)
1. **Who opens a case:** the **OWNER only**. From an open legal referral (N04) or straight from a contract that has overdue installments (no referral needed). One open case per contract.
2. **Who records payments made through the court:** a cashier or collector, through the normal **N03 / N06 collection**. The lawyer never touches cash. No new money path and no link from a payment to the case: the case shows the contract's collections dated on or after the day it was opened.
3. **What a lawyer sees:** **only the cases assigned to them** (and the contract and customer of those cases). The owner sees every case.
4. **Closing a case** records an outcome and changes **nothing** on the contract or the customer. A write-off or early settlement after a judgment is done by hand with N05. Unblocking the customer is done by hand with R01.
5. **Opening a case blocks the customer** from new installment sales (R01 status `blocked`, with a reason). The block stays after the case closes until the owner lifts it.

## Decisions (record them in the decision record)
1. **N09 is folded into L01.** The "sent to legal" status is "the contract has an open legal case". There is no separate flag column: the contract and customer reads compute it from `legal_cases`. EC-88 closes as a duplicate when this merges.
2. **Schema (one new migration).** The tables exist since the baseline. Add only what is missing:
   - `legal_cases.opened_on date NOT NULL` (the shop date the case was opened, `app.org_today`; the "paid since opened" cut-off). Add it as nullable, then `NOT NULL` (the table is empty on dev and prod: check both and report).
   - `legal_cases.outcome_kind text` with `CHECK (outcome_kind IN ('paid', 'settled_out_of_court', 'judgment_enforced', 'withdrawn', 'dismissed_by_court', 'other'))`, plus `CHECK ((status = 'closed') = (outcome_kind IS NOT NULL))`. `outcome` (existing, text ≤ 1000) holds the note; it is required for `other`.
   - `CHECK (length(note) BETWEEN 1 AND 2000)` on `legal_case_notes.note`; `CHECK (length(outcome) <= 500)` on `legal_hearings.outcome` and `length(notes) <= 2000` on `legal_hearings.notes`.
   - RLS on the three tables: no direct reads or writes for `authenticated` (everything goes through the functions below, like N03 / N04). Check what the baseline already grants and say so.
   - The rest stays: the number from `app.next_document_number(org, contract.branch_id, 'legal_case')` (`LC` prefix), `legal_cases_one_open_idx` (one open case per contract), `idempotency_key`.
3. **Who sees a case:** new helper `app.can_see_legal_case(p_lawyer_employee_id uuid)`. True for the OWNER. For anyone else: `legal.view` **and** `p_lawyer_employee_id = app.current_employee_id()` (`app.current_employee_id()`). Every read and write below uses it. A case the caller can't see answers `LEGAL_CASE_NOT_FOUND`, the same as another shop's id.
4. **Who changes a case:** the OWNER, or the assigned lawyer holding `legal.manage`. Opening a case and assigning or changing the lawyer: **OWNER only** (`app.current_role() = 'OWNER'`, as N04's dismiss does).
5. **The lawyer** must be an active employee of the shop with role `LAWYER` (else `LAWYER_INVALID`, 422). The lawyer can be empty while the case is `preparing`. Moving it to `filed` needs a lawyer, a `court` and a `case_number` (else `VALIDATION_FAILED` naming the missing field).
6. **Opening (`public.open_legal_case(p_body jsonb, p_idempotency_key text)`)**, one transaction:
   - Body (strict): `contract_id` (required), `lawyer_employee_id?`, `court?`, `case_type?` (text ≤ 100), `case_number?` (≤ 100), `claim_amount?` (string money), `note?` (≤ 2000, becomes the first case note).
   - Module `legal` (`app.require_module('legal')`). Lock the contract (`FOR UPDATE`). It must be `active` or `rescheduled` **and** have an overdue amount > 0 in `v_contract_balances` **or** an `open` legal referral; else `LEGAL_CONTRACT_NOT_DEFAULTED` (422). An open case on it → `LEGAL_CASE_ALREADY_OPEN` (409, `{ legal_case_id }`).
   - `claim_amount` defaults to the contract's `remaining` now. If given, it must be > 0 and **≤ remaining** (else `LEGAL_CLAIM_TOO_HIGH`, 422, `{ remaining }`). Court costs are never part of it (rule 4).
   - Writes the case (`status = 'preparing'`, `opened_on = app.org_today(org)`, `customer_id` from the contract) and the optional first note. If the contract has an `open` legal referral, it becomes `case_opened` with `legal_case_id` set (N04's CHECK already demands both).
   - If the customer is `active`, set it to `blocked` with the reason "Legal case {number} opened" + audit `customer.status_set` (severity `warning`), the same way N04's job does it (write the status directly, not through `set_customer_status`, which needs `customer.edit`). Already blocked: no change, no second audit.
   - Audit `legal.case_opened` (case id, number, contract, claim amount, referral id or null).
   - Idempotency: the same key + same body returns the same case; the same key + another body → the project's usual idempotency conflict error.
7. **Updating details (`public.update_legal_case(p_id uuid, p_body jsonb)`):** strict body, any of `court`, `case_type`, `case_number`, `claim_amount`, `lawyer_employee_id` (owner only). `claim_amount` can only change while `preparing` (else `LEGAL_CASE_FILED`, 409) and follows the rule in decision 6. A closed case → `LEGAL_CASE_CLOSED` (409). Audit `legal.case_updated` with before and after of the changed fields only.
8. **Status (`public.set_legal_case_status(p_id uuid, p_body jsonb)`):** body `{ status, outcome_kind?, outcome? }`. Allowed moves: `preparing → filed → in_court → judgment`, `judgment → in_court` (appeal), and **any open status → `closed`**. Closing needs `outcome_kind` (and `outcome` for `other`) and sets `closed_at`. Any other move → `LEGAL_STATUS_INVALID` (409, `{ from, to }`). A closed case never reopens: the owner opens a new case (the one-open index allows it). Closing touches nothing outside the case (owner default 4). Audit `legal.case_status_changed`, or `legal.case_closed` on close.
9. **Hearings:**
   - Add: `public.add_legal_hearing(p_case_id uuid, p_body jsonb)` with `{ hearing_date, notes? }`. A past date is allowed (recording history). Only on an open case.
   - Update: `public.update_legal_hearing(p_case_id uuid, p_hearing_id uuid, p_body jsonb)` with any of `{ hearing_date, outcome, notes }`. A hearing of another case → `HEARING_NOT_FOUND` (404). Only on an open case.
   - After every hearing write, set `legal_cases.next_hearing_date` = the earliest hearing on or after the shop's today with no outcome yet (or null). The reads compute the same value live, so a date that passed overnight is never shown as "next".
   - Audit `legal.hearing_added` and `legal.hearing_updated`.
10. **Notes:** `public.add_legal_case_note(p_case_id uuid, p_body jsonb)` with `{ note }`. Notes are append-only: no edit, no delete. Allowed on a closed case too (follow-up after the judgment). Audit `legal.note_added`.
11. **Documents** use F14 as they are: purpose `legal_document`, `entity_type = 'legal_case'`, upload needs `legal.manage`, read needs `legal.view` (`files.rules.ts`). Add one rule: listing files or getting a signed URL for `entity_type = 'legal_case'` also passes `app.can_see_legal_case`, so a lawyer can't open another lawyer's papers. Do it in the same place the list and URL already check the entity, and add the case to the files cross-shop and denial tests. The contract's own signed scans (N07 purposes) stay on the contract.
12. **Payments through the court (owner default 2):** no new write. The case detail returns `amounts`:
    - `claim_amount`, the contract's `total_payable`
    - `paid_before_case` and `paid_since_opened`: effective (not reversed) collection allocations on this contract, split by business date `< opened_on` and `>= opened_on`
    - `remaining` now, from `v_contract_balances`

    It also returns `collections_since_opened`: up to 50 rows (receipt id, number, date, amount on this contract, taken by), newest first. Reuse N03's allocation reads; never recompute amounts another way.
13. **The "legal" flag (N09):** add one key to `app.contract_json` and `app.customer_contract_json` (recreate the latest definitions, everything else unchanged): `legal_case: { id, number, status, opened_on } | null`, the contract's **open** case only. The flag is shown whatever the caller's case visibility is (an open case is a fact about the contract), but the id only opens for those who may see it. Also add `legal_case: { id, number, status } | null` to the rows of `public.list_overdue_contracts` (recreate N06's latest version, same columns plus this one).
14. **The lawyer sees no sales and no cash.** The `LAWYER` role keeps only `legal.view`, `legal.manage`, `customer.view_pii` and `overdue.view` (N04). Assert it in an SQL test, so a future migration that grants it more fails. `get_contract` already lets a lawyer read the contract (`customer_read_guard` accepts `legal.view`, and `can_see_contract` accepts `overdue.view`). The `sale_number` in the contract JSON stays (it is printed on the contract), but the Web shows no link to the sale without a sales permission.
15. **Lawyer pick list:** `public.list_lawyers()` returns the shop's active `LAWYER` employees `{ id, full_name, phone, open_cases }`, for the OWNER only.

## Routes (9), file `src/routes/legal.ts` (thin, like `overdue.ts`), module `legal` on all of them
1. `GET /api/legal/cases?status=open|closed|all&lawyer_id=&q=&limit=&cursor=` → `public.list_legal_cases(p_filters jsonb)`. Needs `legal.view`. Default `open`. `q` matches the case number, court case number, contract number, or customer name or phone. Rows: `{ id, number, case_number, court, case_type, status, opened_on, next_hearing_date, claim_amount, remaining, contract: { id, number, branch_id }, customer: { id, name, phone, status }, lawyer: { id, name } | null }`. Order: open cases with the soonest next hearing first (nulls last), then `opened_on DESC, id`. Keyset cursor, `limit` 1-100 (default 25). A lawyer only gets their own cases; `lawyer_id` is ignored for them.
2. `GET /api/legal/cases/:id` → `public.get_legal_case(p_id)`. The row above plus `guarantors` (from the contract), `hearings` (all, by date), `notes` (newest first, with the author's name), `amounts` and `collections_since_opened` (decision 12), `referral: { id, business_date, days_late } | null`, `outcome_kind`, `outcome`, `closed_at`, `created_by_name`.
3. `POST /api/legal/cases` (header `Idempotency-Key`) → `open_legal_case` (decision 6). 201 with the case detail.
4. `PATCH /api/legal/cases/:id` → `update_legal_case` (decision 7).
5. `POST /api/legal/cases/:id/status` → `set_legal_case_status` (decision 8).
6. `POST /api/legal/cases/:id/hearings` → `add_legal_hearing`.
7. `PATCH /api/legal/cases/:id/hearings/:hearingId` → `update_legal_hearing`.
8. `POST /api/legal/cases/:id/notes` → `add_legal_case_note`.
9. `GET /api/legal/lawyers` → `list_lawyers` (owner).

Every write answers with the full case detail (route 2's shape), so the Web replaces the case in its cache.

New errors (add them to the contracts error list and the API error map): `LEGAL_CASE_NOT_FOUND` (404), `HEARING_NOT_FOUND` (404), `LEGAL_CASE_ALREADY_OPEN` (409), `LEGAL_CASE_CLOSED` (409), `LEGAL_CASE_FILED` (409), `LEGAL_STATUS_INVALID` (409), `LEGAL_CONTRACT_NOT_DEFAULTED` (422), `LEGAL_CLAIM_TOO_HIGH` (422), `LAWYER_INVALID` (422).

## Done when
- **A case links to its contract and amounts:** opening from a referral and opening from a contract both write one case with the contract, customer and claim (= remaining by default). The referral becomes `case_opened` with the case id. The detail's guarantors equal the contract's, and `paid_before_case + paid_since_opened` equals the contract's paid amount.
- **Owed never changes:** `v_contract_balances` (total, paid, remaining) for the contract is identical before opening, after every case write and after closing (SQL test, row by row). No row is written to `payments`, `schedule_lines`, `charges` or any cash or ledger table by any legal function.
- **One open case:** a second open on the same contract → `LEGAL_CASE_ALREADY_OPEN` with the first id. After closing, a new case can be opened. A contract with nothing overdue and no open referral → `LEGAL_CONTRACT_NOT_DEFAULTED`.
- **Claim:** a claim above remaining → `LEGAL_CLAIM_TOO_HIGH`, nothing written. The claim can't change after `filed`.
- **Status:** every allowed move works. `preparing → judgment`, `closed → anything`, and `filed` without a lawyer, court or case number are refused. Closing without an outcome kind is refused.
- **Customer block:** opening blocks an `active` customer with one audit. An already blocked customer gets no second audit. An N02 installment sale for them is refused `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`. Closing the case doesn't unblock.
- **A lawyer can't see sales or cash:** for a `LAWYER` employee, `GET` on the sales, sales history, returns, quotations, shifts, cash accounts, cash transfers, expenses and reports routes → `PERMISSION_DENIED`. The SQL test asserts the role's exact permission list (decision 14).
- **A lawyer sees only their cases:** the list returns only their cases. Another lawyer's case id → `LEGAL_CASE_NOT_FOUND` on the read, every write and the case's files (list and signed URL).
- **Isolation:** shop B's case, hearing and contract ids → not found for every route. A shop without the `legal` module → `MODULE_NOT_IN_PLAN`.
- **Idempotency:** the same open request twice → one case, the same answer.
- All local checks green: API unit, contracts, SQL files, integration, the full API CI recipe, and Web unit + the **whole** e2e suite (not only the new test), as for D03.

## Out of scope
- Court costs, lawyer fees or any charge on the customer: never, rule 4. The shop's own legal costs are recorded as ordinary expenses, with no link to the case.
- Hearing reminders and notifications (N08 / Z01).
- Lawyers from outside the shop without a login: record them as a `LAWYER` employee.
- A write-off or settlement from a judgment (N05, by hand).
- Reopening a closed case.
- A legal report (D-slices).
- Editing or deleting notes.
