## Goal
One sale can be paid with more than one method: cash into the shift's drawer, a bank transfer into a bank account with its reference, and the customer's store credit. The lines must add up to the sale total exactly. Each line posts to its own ledger, the cashier sees the change to give back, and the shift shows what it took by method.

## Slice text (S02)
- **Includes:** payment lines on a sale: cash, bank transfer, store credit; down payment + installment contract (N02); card payments are V2 (Y01); a bank transfer is recorded with a reference; totals must equal the sale total exactly; change given for cash is calculated; each payment line posts to the right ledger (drawer, vault, customer balance).
- **Done when:** a sale paid half cash, half bank transfer posts each amount to the right place and the day close (K04) shows both.

## Decisions (record them in D64)
1. **The body gains `payments`** (1–5 lines). Without it the sale is paid in cash in full, exactly as in S01, so S01's clients keep working.
2. **Methods and limits per sale:** at most one `cash` line, at most one `store_credit` line, up to three `bank_transfer` lines (two different banks, or two transfers into one). Card is V2: not in the enum, refused by the schema.
3. **Exact total:** Σ `amount` = the sale total, to the piastre → new `PAYMENT_TOTAL_MISMATCH` (400, details `{ total, paid }`). Every amount is above 0.
4. **Cash:** goes into the shift's drawer (S01's step). The line may carry `tendered` (what the customer handed over, ≥ `amount`); the server stores it and answers `change = tendered − amount`. Only `amount` is posted to the ledger: the change never enters the drawer. `tendered` below `amount` → `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
5. **Bank transfer:** `cash_account_id` is an **active bank** account of the sale's branch or a shop-wide one (`VAULT_INVALID` otherwise, also for a safe or drawer; `CASH_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE`), and `reference` is required (trimmed, 1–100). One `cash_movements` row: type `sale`, `+amount`, on the bank, **no** `shift_id` (K02's trigger keeps shift rows on the drawer), source `sale`, note = the sale number and the reference. The `payments` row keeps `shift_id` so the shift can report it.
6. **Store credit:** needs the sale's `customer_id` (`VALIDATION_FAILED` without one). The customer's balance must cover the amount → `INSUFFICIENT_STORE_CREDIT` (409, details `{ balance }`; the code exists). One `store_credit_movements` row: `-amount`, source `sale`. The `payments` row has no cash account. (Nothing creates store credit before S05's returns; the tests seed a balance directly.)
7. **One row per line:** a `payments` row (`direction in`, number from `next_document_number(org, branch, 'payment')`, idempotency key `system:sale:<sale id>:<n>`) and one `payment_allocations` row to the sale. Payment lines are posted in the order given.
8. **New column:** `payments.tendered numeric(18,2)`, nullable, CHECK `tendered IS NULL OR (method = 'cash' AND tendered >= amount)`. This is the only schema change; receipts (S04) print it.
9. **Bank accounts for a cashier:** a cashier does not hold `cash.view`, so a new read `GET /api/sales/payment-accounts` (`sale.create`) lists the active banks the caller's open shift can take a transfer into, by **name only** (no balances), like K02's vault list. No open shift → an empty list.
10. **The shift shows what it took (the part K04 will read):** `Shift` and `ShiftSummary` (K02) gain `sales_total` and `payment_totals: { cash, bank_transfer, store_credit }` = the incoming payments of the shift by method. Cash there equals the drawer's `sale` movements. K04's day close sums the same rows per branch and day; until K04 exists, the Done when is checked on the shift.
11. **Quote:** `POST /api/sales/quote` accepts the same `payments` and answers `paid`, `remaining` and `change`, with the same validation except the money checks that need locks (the store credit balance is reported, not reserved).
12. **Down payment + contract (N02) is not built here**, but the payment step must not block it: `app.post_sale_payments` takes the amount it must cover as a parameter (S02 passes the sale total; N02 will pass the down payment).
13. **Audit:** still one `sale.created` row; its `after` gains the payment lines (method, amount, account, reference).
14. **No edits:** a posted payment is never changed or deleted. Refunds are S05, void is S07.

## Database facts (baseline; S02 adds one column)
- `payments`: `number, branch_id, direction, method payment_method (cash, bank_transfer, store_credit), amount > 0, customer_id, cash_account_id, shift_id, reference, received_by, paid_at, business_date, idempotency_key`; CHECKs `(method = 'store_credit') = (cash_account_id IS NULL)`, `method <> 'store_credit' OR customer_id IS NOT NULL`, `method <> 'bank_transfer' OR reference IS NOT NULL`.
- `payment_allocations`: exactly one target (`sale_id` here), `amount > 0`.
- `store_credit_movements` (append-only: `customer_id, amount <> 0, source_type, source_id, branch_id, business_date`) and the trigger-kept `store_credit_balances` (CHECK `balance >= 0`); `app.ledger_mismatches()` already checks this ledger.
- `cash_movements`: a bank can go negative only with `org_settings.allow_negative_bank`; an incoming row never fails that check.
- K01's `app.payable_cash_account(org, id, branch)` accepts a safe **or** a bank: S02 needs banks only, so check the kind too.

## Contract (`packages/contracts/src/sales.ts`; contracts 0.33.0)
- `SalePaymentMethod = z.enum(['cash', 'bank_transfer', 'store_credit'])`.
- `SalePaymentInput`: a discriminated union on `method`, each strict:
  - `{ method: 'cash', amount: PositiveMoney, tendered?: PositiveMoney }` (refine: `tendered ≥ amount`);
  - `{ method: 'bank_transfer', amount: PositiveMoney, cash_account_id: Uuid, reference: string (trim 1–100) }`;
  - `{ method: 'store_credit', amount: PositiveMoney }`.
- `SaleCreateBody` and `SaleQuoteBody` gain `payments?: SalePaymentInput[]` (1–5; at most one cash, one store credit, three bank transfers; store credit needs `customer_id`). `SaleCreateBody` also refines Σ amount = `expected_total`.
- `SalePayment` gains `tendered: Money | null`, `change: Money | null`. `Sale` gains `paid_total`, `change_total`. `SaleQuote` gains `paid`, `remaining`, `change` and `store_credit_balance: Money | null`.
- `SalePaymentAccount`: `{ id, name }`; `SalePaymentAccountList = z.object({ items: z.array(SalePaymentAccount) })`.
- `Shift` / `ShiftSummary` gain `sales_total: Money` and `payment_totals: { cash, bank_transfer, store_credit }` (all `Money`, `"0.00"` when none).
- `PaymentTotalMismatchDetail`: `{ total, paid }`; `StoreCreditDetail`: `{ balance }`.
- New error code: `PAYMENT_TOTAL_MISMATCH` (400). Register both details in `DETAIL_SCHEMAS`. Reuse `INSUFFICIENT_STORE_CREDIT`, `VAULT_INVALID`, `CASH_ACCOUNT_INACTIVE`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
